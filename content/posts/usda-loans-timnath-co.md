## What Is a USDA Loan and How Does It Work?

A USDA loan is backed by the United States Department of Agriculture. Despite the name, you do not need to be a farmer or live on a farm to qualify. The program exists to help moderate-income families buy homes in smaller communities and eligible areas along the Front Range and beyond. It is one of only two mortgage programs that offer zero down payment to most buyers. The other is a VA loan, which is reserved for veterans and active-duty military members.

USDA loans come with competitive interest rates. They also carry a mortgage insurance fee, which is a monthly cost that protects the lender if payments stop. That fee is typically lower than what you would pay with an FHA loan, making USDA one of the more affordable zero-down options available today.

## Who Can Qualify for a USDA Loan?

USDA loans have two main requirements beyond the property location.

The first is income. The USDA sets income limits based on your household size and the county where you are buying. In Larimer County, those limits are designed to help moderate-income households, not just low-income ones. Many families who think they earn too much are surprised to find they actually qualify.

The second is creditworthiness. Most lenders look for a credit score of 640 or higher for a USDA loan, though some flexibility exists depending on your full financial picture. You also need stable employment and a manageable amount of existing debt compared to your income.

USDA loans are for primary residences only. You cannot use this program to buy a vacation home or an investment property.

## How USDA Loans Compare to Other Low-Down Options

You have a few paths if you want to buy with little or nothing down.

A VA loan offers zero down payment but requires military service. An FHA loan requires at least 3.5% down and carries its own mortgage insurance costs. A conventional loan can go as low as 3% down but often requires stronger credit to get the best rates.

A USDA loan stands out because it combines zero down payment with lower mortgage insurance costs than FHA. If you qualify based on location and income, it is often the most affordable path to homeownership for buyers in Timnath and the surrounding Larimer County area.

One thing to keep in mind is that USDA loans are tied to the appraised value of the home, not a hard dollar cap like some other programs. The home must appraise at or above the purchase price.
