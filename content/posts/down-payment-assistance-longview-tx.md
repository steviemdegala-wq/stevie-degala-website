## What Is Down Payment Assistance?

Down payment assistance is money that helps you cover the upfront cost of buying a home. Most home loans require you to bring some cash to the table when you close. That cash is your down payment. Assistance programs provide grants (money you do not have to repay) or second loans (money you pay back slowly or only when you sell) to help you cover that amount. Some programs also help with closing costs, which are the fees you pay at the end of the transaction.

## State Programs Available to Longview Buyers

Texas has two strong programs that help buyers with down payments.

The Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation, known as TSAHC, offers a down payment grant worth up to 5% of your loan amount. You do not have to pay it back. There are two versions of this program. One is reserved for teachers, firefighters, police officers, and veterans. The other is open to anyone who qualifies based on income and home price limits.

The My First Texas Home program is another option. It offers a 30 year fixed rate mortgage combined with a second loan of up to 5% that you can use for your down payment or closing costs. The second loan does not come due unless you sell or refinance.

Both programs work with FHA, VA, and conventional loans, so there is a good chance one fits the type of financing you already have in mind.

## What You Typically Need to Qualify

Each program has its own rules, but most share similar requirements.

You usually need a credit score of at least 620, though some programs accept 640 or higher. Your income needs to fall within certain limits based on the area and your household size. The home you are buying must meet price limits as well. Most programs require you to take a short homebuyer education class before you close. That class walks you through the basics of owning a home and takes just a few hours to complete.

Do not let these requirements scare you off before you check. Many buyers assume they earn too much or their credit is too low, and then discover they qualify just fine.

## How to Stack Programs for Maximum Help

Here is something many buyers do not realize. You can sometimes combine programs. For example, you might pair an FHA loan (which only requires 3.5% down) with a TSAHC grant to cover that entire down payment amount. The result is a home purchase with little or nothing out of pocket besides your closing costs. And if you also qualify for seller concessions on those closing costs, you could walk into your new home in Longview with very little cash spent.

Not every combination is allowed under every program, so it helps to work through the numbers with someone who knows which programs play well together.

**Does using down payment assistance hurt my chances of getting a home?**
No. Sellers typically cannot tell whether you used an assistance program. As long as your offer is competitive and your financing is solid, using assistance does not put you at a disadvantage.

**Do I have to be a first time buyer to qualify?**
Not always. Some programs are open to repeat buyers. The TSAHC Home Sweet Texas option does not require first time buyer status as long as you meet income and price limits.
