Loveland sits in the heart of the Northern Colorado Front Range — close enough to Fort Collins to access its employers and amenities, far enough away to offer meaningfully more home for the dollar. For medical professionals buying in Larimer County, Loveland is often where the math works best: lower purchase prices than Fort Collins proper, strong community infrastructure, and direct access to two major healthcare systems that anchor the local economy.

A [physician loan](/loans/doctor-loan) changes the calculus further. For healthcare professionals who have spent years in training and are entering the workforce with significant student debt, the physician loan's zero-down structure and favorable student debt treatment often means the difference between qualifying now and waiting another two to three years.

## The Loveland Market: What Medical Professionals Are Buying

Loveland's housing market typically runs 10 to 20% below comparable Fort Collins properties — a spread that compresses when you account for quality and location, but remains meaningful. Single-family homes in the $425,000 to $650,000 range represent the primary target for early-career medical professionals entering the market.

At $525,000, a physician loan with zero down versus a conventional loan at 5% down looks like this:

- **Physician loan:** $0 down, no PMI, competitive rate
- **Conventional at 5%:** $26,250 down, PMI of roughly $160–$225 per month until reaching 80% LTV

For a physician or advanced practice provider who is also managing student loan payments, that $26,250 plus the $200/month in PMI has a real opportunity cost. The physician loan keeps that capital available for loan paydown, retirement contribution, or an emergency fund that would otherwise be built from scratch.

## McKee Medical Center and the UCHealth Network

Loveland's primary healthcare anchor is **Banner Health McKee Medical Center** on Garfield Avenue — a full-service regional hospital with a broad employed physician and APP base. Medical professionals joining McKee's staff often look to Loveland first for housing, both for the proximity and because the city's pace and character tends to appeal to the professional cohort attracted to community-based hospital medicine.

The **UCHealth Medical Center of the Rockies** in neighboring Johnstown (just east of Loveland along Highway 34) draws physicians and advanced practice providers from across the broader Loveland/Berthoud/Windsor corridor. For staff there, Loveland represents a logical base — accessible by a short drive without the price premium of Fort Collins.

Both systems attract the borrower profile that physician loans were designed for: strong income trajectory, significant training debt, and early-career professionals who have not had the time to accumulate a large down payment.

## Student Loan Debt and Loveland's Price Range

For a physician buying in Loveland with $200,000+ in student loans, the conventional loan calculation can be punishing. Under standard Fannie Mae guidelines, if you are not in repayment or your payment does not appear on your credit report, lenders use 1% of the total balance as the assumed monthly payment — $2,000/month added to your debt load before the new mortgage is even factored in.

At Loveland's price range, that level of assumed student debt can reduce qualifying purchase price by $200,000 to $250,000 under conventional guidelines — enough to move you from a comfortable home in a good neighborhood to something that requires significant compromise.

A physician loan handles this differently. Most programs exclude deferred student loans from DTI entirely. Others use your actual income-driven repayment payment. The difference in qualifying income that this creates is often the determining factor in whether a medical professional can buy now or has to wait.

## Residents and New Attendings: Relocating to Loveland

If you are completing a residency or fellowship and have accepted a position at McKee, UCHealth, or another Loveland-area employer, a physician loan may allow you to buy before your start date. A fully executed employment contract replaces W-2s and pay stubs as income documentation on most physician loan programs.

This is particularly valuable for medical professionals relocating from another city or state. Renting temporarily while house-hunting is one option — but for professionals arriving with an employment contract and a clear start date, buying before the first paycheck saves the cost and disruption of a double move.

## Loveland vs. Fort Collins for Physician Loan Buyers

The honest comparison:

**Fort Collins** offers more walkability, a stronger restaurant and culture scene, and more established neighborhoods. Home prices run higher — $50,000 to $150,000 more for comparable homes in many cases. For buyers whose loan amount is already at the top of physician loan program limits, Fort Collins can push into jumbo territory.

**Loveland** offers more space for the dollar, newer inventory in many areas, strong outdoor access (Horseshoe Reservoir, Loveland Ski Area nearby, direct mountain access), and a quieter pace that suits many healthcare professionals who spend long hours at work and want home to feel like a genuine retreat.

For physician loan buyers whose priorities include maximizing what they get for their monthly payment while keeping capital for other financial goals, Loveland typically wins the comparison.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do physician loans work for existing homes in Loveland, not just new construction?**
Yes. Physician loans are available for existing home purchases, not just new builds. The vast majority of physician loan transactions I handle in Loveland are on existing homes — the same qualification advantages apply regardless of the home's age.

**Can a Nurse Practitioner or PA use a physician loan to buy in Loveland?**
Yes. Physician loan programs have expanded beyond physicians in most cases. NPs, PAs, CRNAs, RNs, and other advanced practice credentials qualify under most programs I work with. The label is broader than it sounds.

**What is the minimum down payment for a physician loan in Loveland?**
Most programs allow 0% down up to $2M loan amount. At Loveland's typical price range ($425,000–$650,000), zero down is available on virtually all physician loan programs for qualifying borrowers with 680+ credit.

**Can I use a physician loan if I am buying in Loveland but plan to commute to Fort Collins or Greeley?**
Yes. The loan program does not care where you work — only that the property is your primary residence and that you hold a qualifying credential. Many physicians in this market live in Loveland and commute across the broader Front Range.
