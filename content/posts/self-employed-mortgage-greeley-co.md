Getting a self-employed mortgage in Greeley CO is very possible. The process looks different than a standard W-2 application, but Weld County has a large population of business owners, agricultural workers, independent contractors, and self-employed professionals — and there are solid loan programs designed for exactly this kind of borrower.

Here is what you need to know before you apply.

## Why Self-Employed Income Works Differently in the Mortgage Process

When you get a paycheck from an employer, lenders can verify your income with a pay stub and a W-2. When you run your own business or work as a contractor, lenders have to dig deeper.

The core challenge is that tax returns often understate income for self-employed borrowers. Business deductions — equipment, home office, vehicles, travel — reduce your taxable income, which is the point of those deductions. But lenders use that lower taxable number to calculate how much house you can afford. The more aggressively you write off expenses, the larger the gap between your real income and the number a bank sees.

Understanding that gap is the first step to finding the right loan structure.

## Bank Statement Loans: Built for Self-Employed Greeley Borrowers

A bank statement loan skips the tax return entirely. Instead, the lender looks at 12 to 24 months of your bank deposits and averages them out. That average becomes your qualifying income.

For self-employed buyers in Greeley who have strong cash flow but heavy write-offs, this is often the single most effective tool available. You will typically need a credit score of 620 or higher and a down payment between 10 and 20 percent. Rates are slightly higher than conventional loans, but the difference in qualifying power often makes it well worth it.

## Conventional Loans for Self-Employed Borrowers in Weld County

If your tax returns do show enough income to qualify, a conventional loan gives you access to better rates and more program options. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac allow self-employed borrowers with at least two years of documented self-employment history to use that income to qualify.

Greeley and Weld County fall within standard conforming loan limits, so the full range of conventional programs is available here. You will need two years of personal tax returns, two years of business returns if applicable, a year-to-date profit and loss statement, and a debt-to-income ratio within program guidelines. Down payments can start as low as three to five percent.

## Greeley's Economy and What It Means for Self-Employed Buyers

Greeley is the county seat of Weld County and has a diverse economic base. Agriculture, oil and gas, manufacturing, and the University of Northern Colorado all drive employment here. That mix creates a lot of self-employed income — farmers, contractors, consultants, small business owners, and tradespeople make up a significant portion of the Greeley workforce.

Home prices in Greeley are generally more affordable than in Fort Collins or Denver, which gives self-employed buyers more room to work with. The market has grown steadily, and newer developments on the east and south sides of the city offer good inventory options.

Having a preapproval ready before you shop is important here. Self-employed applications take a few extra days to pull together compared to W-2 applications, so getting your documents organized early puts you in a stronger position when you find the right home.

## Documents to Have Ready

For a bank statement loan: 12 to 24 months of personal or business bank statements, a profit and loss statement from a CPA, and proof of self-employment such as a business license.

For a conventional or FHA loan: two years of personal tax returns, two years of business tax returns if applicable, a year-to-date profit and loss statement, and recent bank statements.

## FAQ: Self-Employed Mortgage in Greeley CO

**How long do I need to be self-employed before I can apply?**

Two years is the standard for conventional and FHA loans. Some bank statement loan programs accept one year if you have relevant prior experience in the same field. Your broker will tell you exactly where you stand.

**My tax return shows low income because of deductions. Am I stuck?**

Not at all. A bank statement loan uses your actual deposits, not your taxable income. If your cash flow is healthy, that program can be a much better fit than anything that relies on your tax return.

**What credit score do I need?**

Conventional loans typically require a minimum of 620. FHA loans allow scores as low as 580. Bank statement loans vary by lender but generally start around 620. A stronger score always improves your rate.

**Can I combine W-2 income and self-employment income to qualify?**

Yes. If you have both, a lender can use both income streams together to build your qualifying picture. This is common for people who work a part-time job alongside a business.

**Are DSCR loans an option in Greeley?**

Yes. If you are buying an investment property in Greeley, a DSCR loan qualifies you based on the rental income the property generates rather than your personal income. It is a popular option for self-employed real estate investors who have significant personal deductions.
