## What Are Investment Property Loans?

An investment property loan is a mortgage you use to buy a home you plan to rent out or hold as an asset rather than live in yourself. Lenders treat these loans differently than the mortgage you would use on your primary home. Because a rental property is seen as a business, lenders typically require a larger down payment and a stronger financial profile.

Here is what that usually looks like in practice. Most conventional investment property loans require at least 15 to 25 percent down. Your credit score will matter more than it does on a regular home purchase. And lenders will look closely at your existing debt load to make sure you can handle another mortgage on top of what you already carry.

## Types of Investment Property Loans Available in Timnath

There is more than one path to financing an investment property. The right option depends on your situation.

**Conventional investment loans** are backed by Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac. They tend to offer competitive interest rates, but they come with stricter qualification standards. You will need good credit and a solid debt to income ratio. Debt to income is the percentage of your monthly gross income that goes toward debt payments. Most lenders want that number below 45 percent.

**DSCR loans** stand for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. This type of loan qualifies you based on the rental income the property is expected to generate rather than your personal income. If you are self employed or have income that is hard to document, a DSCR loan can be a great fit. The rental income just needs to cover the mortgage payment at a ratio the lender sets, usually 1.0 or higher.

**Portfolio loans** are held by the lender instead of being sold to investors. They can offer more flexibility on qualification, especially if you own several properties already.

**Hard money loans** are short term loans often used by investors who plan to renovate and resell a property quickly. Rates are higher, but the approval process is faster.

## How to Qualify for an Investment Property Loan in Timnath

Here is a straightforward checklist of what lenders typically look at.

Your credit score should be at least 620 for most conventional loans. A score of 700 or higher will get you better rates. Your down payment will be at least 15 percent on a single unit property and up to 25 percent on a two to four unit property. Lenders want to see cash reserves, which means money left in your account after closing. Six months of mortgage payments is a common requirement.

Your existing rental income can sometimes be counted as part of your qualifying income. Your lender will want a lease agreement or a market rent analysis from an appraiser to use those numbers.

If you already own investment properties, lenders will want to know how many. Fannie Mae allows up to 10 financed properties, but qualifying gets more involved as that number goes up.

## FAQ

**How much do I need to put down on an investment property in Timnath?**
Most conventional loans require between 15 and 25 percent down depending on the property type. A single family rental usually requires 15 to 20 percent. A two to four unit property typically requires 25 percent.

**Can rental income help me qualify for the loan?**
Yes. In many cases, the expected rental income from the property can be used to help you qualify. Your lender will want documentation, such as a signed lease or an appraisal that includes a rent estimate.

**Are investment property interest rates higher than primary home rates?**
Yes. Investment property loans typically carry interest rates that are 0.5 to 1 percent higher than rates on a primary residence. This reflects the additional risk lenders take on when the property is not owner occupied.

**What is a DSCR loan and is it a good fit for investors?**
A DSCR loan qualifies you based on rental income rather than your personal income. It is a great fit if you are self employed, have complex finances, or want to scale your portfolio without using your personal income to qualify each time.

**Do I need to live in Colorado to buy an investment property in Timnath?**
No. Out of state investors can purchase investment properties in Timnath. Your lender will still want to verify your income, assets, and credit, but you do not need to be a Colorado resident.
