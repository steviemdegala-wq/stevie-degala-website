A mortgage lender is the person or company that funds your home loan. They review your income, credit, and assets, then offer you a loan with a specific rate and terms. ## Loan Types Available from a Fort Collins Mortgage Lender

A good mortgage lender in Fort Collins CO can offer you a wide range of loan programs, including:

**Conventional loans.** These are the most common for buyers with good credit and at least three to five percent down. They often come with lower rates and no mortgage insurance if you put twenty percent down.

**FHA loans.** FHA stands for Federal Housing Administration. These loans allow down payments as low as 3.5 percent and are more flexible on credit scores. They work well for first time buyers.

**VA loans.** If you served in the military, VA loans offer zero down payment and no private mortgage insurance. Fort Collins has a strong veteran community and VA loans are a great fit for many buyers here.

**USDA loans.** Some areas on the outskirts of Fort Collins and in neighboring towns qualify for USDA rural development loans, which also allow zero down.

**Jumbo loans.** For homes above the conventional loan limit, which is around $806,500 in Larimer County for 2026, a jumbo loan covers the difference. Fort Collins has a growing number of homes in this price range.

**DSCR loans.** If you are buying an investment property, a Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan lets you qualify based on the rental income the property generates rather than your personal income.

## What to Look for in a Fort Collins Mortgage Lender

Not all lenders are the same. Here are a few things worth checking when you shop around.

**Local knowledge.** A lender who knows Fort Collins neighborhoods, Larimer County property values, and common local HOA situations will help you avoid surprises.

**Speed and communication.** A good lender picks up the phone. When a seller wants to close in thirty days, your lender needs to deliver.

**Transparent pricing.** Ask for a Loan Estimate and compare the interest rate, APR (the annual percentage rate, which includes fees), and closing costs across lenders before committing.

**Multiple loan options.** A lender who works with many loan programs can find the right fit for your situation rather than pushing you toward one product.

## How to Get Started with a Mortgage Lender in Fort Collins

The first step is a pre-approval. You share basic income, employment, and credit information, and the lender reviews your file to tell you how much you can borrow and at what rate. A pre-approval letter gives you credibility when you make an offer on a home.

You will typically need:

- Two years of tax returns and W2s or profit and loss statements if self employed
- Recent pay stubs or proof of income
- Two to three months of bank statements
- A government issued ID
- Your Social Security number for a credit pull

Pre-approval usually takes just a day or two when you work with a responsive local lender.

## FAQ: Fort Collins Mortgage Lender Questions

**Is a mortgage broker the same as a mortgage lender?**
Not exactly. A mortgage lender funds the loan directly. A mortgage broker, like Stevie De Gala, works with multiple lenders and shops your loan to find the best terms. Many buyers get better deals through a broker because the broker has access to wholesale rates.

**How do I know if I am getting a good mortgage rate in Fort Collins?**
Compare the APR (not just the interest rate) on at least two or three loan estimates. The APR includes fees and gives a more complete picture of what the loan actually costs.

**What credit score do I need to buy a home in Fort Collins?**
Conventional loans typically want a 620 or higher. FHA loans can go as low as 580 with 3.5 percent down. VA loans have no official minimum but most lenders look for 580 to 620. The higher your score, the better your rate will be.

**How long does it take to close on a home in Fort Collins?**
Most purchases close in 20 to 45 days. With a fully prepared file and a responsive lender, closing in three weeks is possible.
