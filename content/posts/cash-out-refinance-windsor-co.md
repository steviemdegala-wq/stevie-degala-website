## What Is a Cash Out Refinance Windsor CO?

A cash out refinance is not a second loan added on top of your first. It is a brand new mortgage that pays off your existing loan and gives you extra funds at closing. Here is a straightforward example. Say your Windsor home is worth $520,000 and you still owe $300,000. You have $220,000 in equity. With a cash out refinance, you might take out a new mortgage for $390,000, pay off the old loan, cover closing costs, and receive roughly $80,000 in cash.

Lenders typically let you borrow up to 80 percent of your home's appraised value on a conventional loan. That 80 percent limit protects you from going too far underwater if values soften. Your lender will order a professional appraisal to confirm your current value before approving anything.

## What to Consider Before a Cash Out Refinance in Windsor CO

The most important thing to understand is that a cash out refinance replaces your entire existing mortgage with a new one at today's interest rate. If your current rate is lower than what is available today, your monthly payment could increase even if you take a modest amount of cash. Running the numbers carefully before you commit is essential.

Closing costs are part of the equation too. They typically run between two and five percent of your new loan amount. Some lenders let you roll those costs into your loan balance so you do not need to bring cash to closing, though that does add to your total amount owed.

Credit score requirements apply just like any other mortgage. Most lenders want to see at least a 620 score. A score of 680 or above will typically unlock better rates and terms.

If you have a VA loan, ask specifically about the VA cash out refinance option. Veterans can often borrow up to 100 percent of their home's value through that program, which is more generous than the conventional 80 percent limit.

## What the Process Looks Like in Windsor

Most cash out refinances take 30 to 45 days from your first conversation to closing day. Here is the short version of how it works.

You start by talking through your goals with a mortgage broker. Your broker will pull your credit, review your current loan details, and give you a loan estimate showing your new rate, payment, and projected closing costs.

From there you submit your application with supporting documents. Those usually include recent pay stubs, two years of tax returns, bank statements, and your current mortgage statement.

The lender orders an appraisal of your Windsor home. The appraiser visits the property, takes photos, and looks at recent sales of comparable homes in Weld County.

Once the appraisal is complete and underwriting approves your file, you get a clear to close. You sign your new loan documents and receive your cash within three business days after closing.

## FAQ: Cash Out Refinance Windsor CO

**How much equity do I need to qualify?**
Most lenders want you to keep at least 20 percent equity in your home after the refinance. That means you can typically borrow up to 80 percent of your appraised value minus what you still owe on your existing loan.

**How long do I need to have owned my home before doing a cash out refinance?**
Most conventional lenders require you to have been on title for at least six to twelve months. If you purchased recently, you may need to wait a bit before you are eligible.

**Will my monthly payment go up?**
That depends on how much cash you take out and what rate you get compared to your current loan. Your mortgage broker can run the numbers so you know exactly what your new payment would be before you commit to anything.

**Can I use cash out funds for any purpose?**
Yes. The money you receive is yours to use however you choose. Lenders do not typically require you to explain or document how you plan to spend it once the loan closes.

**Is the money I receive taxable?**
Loan proceeds are generally not considered taxable income. If you use the funds for home improvements, some of your mortgage interest may be deductible. A tax advisor can help you understand your specific situation.
