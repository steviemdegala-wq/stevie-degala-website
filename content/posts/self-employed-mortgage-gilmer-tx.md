## Why Self-Employed Buyers Face Extra Hurdles

Traditional lenders look at your tax returns to verify income. If you write off a lot of expenses, which most smart business owners do, your taxable income looks lower than your actual earnings. That lower number is what the bank sees. That is the number they use to decide if you qualify.

This is the most common frustration I hear from self-employed buyers in Gilmer and across Upshur County. Your bank account tells one story. Your tax return tells another. Standard loans trust the tax return. That feels unfair, but there are solutions.

## Bank Statement Loans: The Self-Employed Mortgage Option That Works in Gilmer TX

A bank statement loan lets you qualify based on your actual deposits instead of your tax return. Lenders look at 12 to 24 months of bank statements and average out your deposits. That number becomes your qualifying income.

This works especially well if you run a local business in Gilmer. You might own a small shop downtown. You might do construction, farming, or a trade in Upshur County. Your bank statements show real cash coming in, and a bank statement loan uses exactly that.

You will usually need a credit score of 620 or higher. A down payment of 10 to 20 percent is typical. Interest rates tend to run slightly higher than a conventional loan, but the trade is worth it when you would not qualify any other way.

## Other Loan Programs Worth Knowing About

Beyond bank statement loans, a few other programs may fit your situation.

Conventional loans with two years of self-employment history. If you have filed two years of tax returns showing enough income, you may qualify for a conventional loan. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac allow this, and rates are often better than specialty programs.

FHA loans for self-employed buyers. FHA loans have more flexible guidelines than conventional loans. You still need two years of self-employment history, but the down payment can be as low as 3.5 percent. This is a great option if you have solid income but a smaller down payment saved.

DSCR loans for investment properties. If you are buying a rental property in Gilmer or anywhere in Upshur County, a DSCR loan (Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan) does not look at your personal income at all. It qualifies you based on the rental income the property will bring in. This is a favorite among self-employed investors who already have strong personal write-offs.

## What Documents Will You Need

The exact documents depend on which loan you choose. Here is a general idea of what to gather before you apply.

For bank statement loans, you will need 12 to 24 months of personal or business bank statements, a profit and loss statement prepared by a CPA, and proof of self-employment such as a business license or a CPA letter.

For conventional or FHA loans, you will need two years of personal tax returns, two years of business tax returns if you own a business entity, a year-to-date profit and loss statement, and recent bank statements.

Having these ready before you apply makes the whole process faster. Start pulling them together now if you are thinking about buying in the next few months.

## FAQ: Self-Employed Mortgage in Gilmer TX

**Can I get a mortgage if I just started my business?**

Most lenders want to see at least two years of self-employment history. If you just started, you may need to wait or find a lender with more flexible guidelines. Some bank statement loan programs require as little as one year.

**Do I have to use my tax return income to qualify?**

Not with a bank statement loan. That program uses your actual deposits instead. With a conventional or FHA loan, the lender will use your tax return income, which may be lower because of business write-offs.

**Will a low credit score disqualify me?**

Not necessarily. FHA loans allow scores as low as 580. Some bank statement loan programs accept scores around 620. The better your score, the better your rate will be, so it is worth reviewing your credit before you apply.

**How long does it take to close on a self-employed loan?**

A typical timeline is 30 to 45 days. Having all your documents ready upfront can speed things up. Bank statement loans may take a bit longer than conventional loans because of the extra income review involved.

**What if my income varies from month to month?**

Lenders expect that with self-employment. They will average out your income over 12 or 24 months to get a stable qualifying number. Seasonal income or irregular deposits are normal, and most programs account for them.
