Windsor has one of the more interesting fix and flip dynamics in Northern Colorado. It sits in Weld County — which has among the lowest property tax rates in Colorado — while benefiting from the same Front Range appreciation and population growth as Larimer County markets like Fort Collins and Timnath. For fix and flip investors, that combination of lower hold costs and strong buyer demand creates favorable deal math.

An [investor line of credit](/loans/investor-line-of-credit) pre-approved and ready to draw is the tool that lets you execute on Windsor deals fast. Draw for acquisition, draw for renovation, sell, repay, and do it again — without reapplying each time a deal surfaces.

## Windsor's Fix and Flip Market

Windsor's housing stock spans a wide range. The newer developments in the town's northeast corridor and near Raindance attract move-up buyers at strong price points. The older housing stock in Windsor's established core — particularly homes built in the 1970s through 1990s — provides the dated interiors and deferred maintenance that create fix and flip margin.

The two buyer pools that matter most for post-renovation sales in Windsor:

**Move-up buyers from Greeley and Fort Collins.** Windsor is geographically positioned between both cities. Buyers priced out of Old Town Fort Collins or looking for more space than Greeley can offer frequently land in Windsor. A renovated property with updated finishes at a Windsor price point is an easy decision for this buyer.

**New construction spillover demand.** Windsor has active new construction. When new builds have extended delivery timelines or lack available inventory in a specific price band, buyers turn to updated existing homes. A renovated Windsor property competes directly for that demand.

## Why Weld County Matters for Investor Math

Property taxes are a holding cost. In Larimer County (Fort Collins, Timnath, Loveland), effective property tax rates typically run 0.5–0.7% of assessed value. In Weld County, where Windsor sits, effective rates are often lower. On a $450,000 property, that can mean $500–$1,500 less per year in holding costs — which matters on a six-month flip timeline.

Lower hold costs give Windsor deals more margin cushion than comparable Larimer County projects at the same acquisition and renovation budget.

## The Revolving Line Advantage in a Fast Market

Windsor properties at the right price point and condition move quickly. Sellers of distressed or dated properties — estate situations, deferred maintenance, investors offloading — often accept non-contingent offers well below list when they need certainty.

A pre-approved line of credit makes those non-contingent offers possible. Your capital is already approved before you write the contract. You can commit to closing in 10 days without a financing contingency, which is often enough to win the deal at a price that works for your margins.

Hard money requires a new application and approval cycle for each project. If the deal needs to close in two weeks, hard money with its 10–15%+ rate and approval timeline is a structural disadvantage. The line is already there.

## BRRRR Path in Windsor

Windsor's rental demand supports the BRRRR strategy effectively:

1. **Draw** for the acquisition of a dated or distressed Windsor property
2. **Renovate** funded by the line
3. **Rent** — Windsor's proximity to the I-25 corridor and Weld County employment creates consistent rental demand
4. **Refinance** into a [DSCR loan](/loans/dscr) at stabilized value
5. **Repay** the line from the DSCR proceeds
6. **Repeat** — the line resets for the next deal

The Weld County tax advantage makes the rental hold more cash-flow positive, which supports stronger DSCR qualification on the refinance.

## What Windsor Investors Qualify On

The same experience-based qualification as the broader program:

- **Deal track record**: Completed fix and flip or BRRRR projects in Northern Colorado or comparable markets
- **Credit**: 680+ minimum, better terms at 700+
- **Liquidity**: Reserves for holding costs and any gap not covered by the draw
- **Exit clarity**: A credible resale or DSCR refinance path for each deal

First-time investors can qualify for a smaller initial line. Investors with multiple completed projects in Windsor, Greeley, Fort Collins, or other Northern Colorado markets typically qualify for larger facilities.
