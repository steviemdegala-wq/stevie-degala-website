Getting a self-employed mortgage in Windsor CO is more achievable than most people expect. The process works a little differently when you earn income outside of a traditional W-2 job, but the right loan structure makes approval realistic — even if your tax returns do not tell the whole story of what you earn.

Windsor has grown into one of the most desirable communities along the Northern Colorado Front Range. If you are self-employed and want to put down roots here, here is what you need to know.

## How Lenders Look at Self-Employment Income

When a W-2 employee applies for a mortgage, income verification takes minutes. When you own a business, do contract work, or earn income as a freelancer, the process takes a few more steps.

Lenders want two things: proof that your income is real, and confidence that it will continue. They typically use two years of federal tax returns, a profit and loss statement, and sometimes business bank statements to build that picture.

The common frustration is that business deductions reduce your taxable income — which is smart tax planning, but it also reduces the income a lender will count toward your mortgage. That gap between what you earn and what your return shows is where self-employed borrowers often run into trouble with traditional banks.

## Bank Statement Loans for Windsor CO Self-Employed Buyers

A bank statement loan works around the tax return problem entirely. Instead of using your taxable income, the lender averages your actual bank deposits over 12 to 24 months. If your cash flow is strong, this number will typically be higher than what your tax return shows — and that is the number that gets you qualified.

This is one of the most useful tools available to self-employed borrowers in Windsor right now. You will generally need a credit score around 620 or higher and a down payment of 10 to 20 percent. Interest rates run slightly above conventional loans, but the trade-off is access to a program that works when others do not.

## Conventional Loans for Self-Employed Borrowers in Weld and Larimer County

If your tax returns do reflect enough qualifying income, a conventional loan often gives you the best rates and the most flexibility. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac both allow self-employed borrowers with at least two years of documented self-employment to qualify.

Windsor straddles the border of Weld and Larimer counties, and both counties fall within standard conforming loan limits. For a conventional loan here, you will need two years of personal and business tax returns, a year-to-date profit and loss statement, and a debt-to-income ratio within program guidelines. Down payments can start as low as three percent on some programs.

## Documents to Have Ready Before You Apply

For a bank statement loan: 12 to 24 months of bank statements, a profit and loss statement from a CPA, and proof of self-employment such as a business license or CPA letter.

For a conventional loan: two years of personal tax returns, two years of business tax returns if applicable, a year-to-date profit and loss statement, and recent bank statements.

Having these organized before you reach out to a lender speeds up the process and avoids surprises mid-application.

## FAQ: Self-Employed Mortgage in Windsor CO

**Do I need two full years of self-employment history?**

For most conventional and FHA programs, yes. Some bank statement loan programs allow one year if you had relevant experience in the same field before going out on your own. Your broker can assess your specific timeline.

**Can I qualify if my income varies from year to year?**

Lenders look for consistency or an upward trend. If your income grew from year one to year two, that is a positive story in underwriting. If it dropped significantly, you may need additional documentation to explain it.

**What credit score do I need for a bank statement loan?**

Most programs start around 620, though requirements vary by lender. A score above 700 opens up better rate options. It is worth reviewing your credit before you apply and addressing anything that can be improved quickly.

**Does Windsor's location in two counties affect my loan options?**

Not significantly. Both Weld and Larimer counties are in standard conforming loan limit territories, so conventional loan limits apply. Your broker will use the county where the property sits for any county-specific calculations.

**How long does closing usually take?**

Typically 30 to 45 days from application to closing. Having your documents ready upfront and responding quickly to any underwriter requests keeps things on track.
