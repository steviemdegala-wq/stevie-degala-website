## What Is a Jumbo Loan in Fort Collins CO?

Every year, the federal government sets a ceiling on how large a home loan can be and still be sold to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac are the two big companies that purchase most mortgages from lenders across the country. Loans within that ceiling are called conforming loans. Loans above it are called jumbo loans.

In Fort Collins and across Larimer County, the conforming limit for a single family home is $806,500 in 2025. If you need to borrow more than that amount, you are looking at a jumbo loan. Lenders who offer jumbo loans take on more risk because they cannot sell these loans to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac. That is why the qualifying standards are a bit stricter.

## Jumbo Loan Requirements: What You Need to Qualify

Because lenders take on more risk with jumbo loans, they ask more from you as a borrower. Here is what most jumbo programs require.

### Credit Score

Most jumbo lenders want to see a credit score of 700 or higher. Some programs prefer 720 or above. The stronger your score, the better your rate will be.

### Down Payment

Many jumbo programs require 10 to 20 percent down. Some programs allow as little as 10 percent if your credit score and income are strong. Putting more down will usually get you a better rate and lower your monthly payment.

### Debt to Income Ratio

This is the percentage of your monthly gross income that goes toward debt payments. Include things like your future mortgage, car payments, and credit card minimums. Most jumbo lenders want this number at or below 43 percent. Some prefer it closer to 38 percent.

### Cash Reserves

Many lenders want to see 6 to 12 months of mortgage payments sitting in your bank or investment accounts after closing. This shows you have a cushion if something changes in your life.

### Income Documentation

Expect to provide two years of tax returns, recent pay stubs, and two to three months of bank statements. If you are self employed, you will need those same items plus a profit and loss statement prepared by a CPA or bookkeeper.

## How Jumbo Loan Rates Compare to Conventional Rates

For most of mortgage history, jumbo loans carried higher interest rates than conforming loans. That spread has narrowed a lot in recent years. Today, jumbo rates are sometimes very close to conventional rates, and occasionally even slightly lower for well qualified buyers.

Your rate will depend on your credit score, your down payment, the loan amount, and the lender you choose. Shopping around matters more with jumbo loans than with conventional ones because there is more variation between lenders. Working with a mortgage broker means someone shops multiple lenders for you at once, which can save you real money over the life of a large loan.

## How to Get a Jumbo Loan in Fort Collins

Getting a jumbo loan starts with the same steps as any other mortgage. Here is a simple way to think about the process.

First, check your credit and income. Pull your credit report and make sure there are no surprises. Add up your monthly debts and compare them to your gross income to get a rough sense of your debt to income ratio.

Second, save for your down payment and reserves. Make sure you have enough for 10 to 20 percent down plus closing costs, and that you still have 6 to 12 months of reserves left over after everything closes.

Third, gather your documents early. Tax returns, pay stubs, and bank statements are the basics. Having these ready before you apply speeds up the whole process.

Fourth, work with a broker. A mortgage broker works with multiple jumbo lenders and can find the program that fits your specific situation. This step often saves buyers thousands of dollars in rate differences alone.

Most programs require a score of at least 700. A score of 720 or higher gives you access to the best rates and the widest range of program options.

**Can I get a jumbo loan with 10 percent down?**

Yes. Some programs allow 10 percent down with strong credit and income. Putting 20 percent down will typically give you a lower rate and eliminates private mortgage insurance.

They can be, but the gap is much smaller than it used to be. In some cases, jumbo rates and conventional rates are nearly the same. Shopping around with a broker is the best way to find the most competitive option for your situation.

Most jumbo loans close in 30 to 45 days, similar to a conventional loan. Having your documents organized and ready ahead of time keeps the process moving smoothly.
