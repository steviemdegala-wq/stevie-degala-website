## What Is a Refinance and Why Do Homeowners in Windsor Use It?

A refinance means replacing your existing mortgage with a new one. The new loan pays off your current balance, and then you start making payments on the new loan going forward.

There are two main types.

A rate and term refinance changes your interest rate, your loan length, or both. Most people do this to lower their monthly payment or to shorten their loan so they build equity faster.

A cash out refinance lets you borrow more than you currently owe. The difference comes to you as cash at closing, and you can use it however you choose. Home improvements, paying off higher interest debt, or covering a big expense are all common reasons Windsor homeowners go this route.

## What You Need to Qualify for a Refinance

Qualifying for a refinance works a lot like qualifying for a home purchase. Lenders look at a handful of key items.

Your credit score. Most conventional loan refinances want to see a credit score of at least 620. The higher your score, the better the rate options available to you.

Your debt to income ratio. This is the share of your monthly gross income that goes toward debt payments each month. Most lenders want this number below 45 percent, though the exact limit can vary depending on the loan type.

Your home equity. For a standard rate and term refinance, lenders usually want you to have at least 20 percent equity remaining after closing. For a cash out refinance, most programs limit you to borrowing up to 80 percent of your home's current appraised value.

Your income history. Lenders want to see two years of consistent, verifiable income. Whether you are a salaried employee, hourly worker, or self employed, there are proper ways to document your income and make the file work.

## What the Refinance Process Looks Like Step by Step

Most refinances in Windsor take about 30 to 45 days from the day you apply to the day you close.

You start by filling out an application and submitting documents like recent pay stubs, tax returns, and bank statements. Your lender pulls your credit and orders a home appraisal to determine what your property is worth today.

Once the appraisal comes in, the file moves to underwriting. The underwriter reviews everything and makes the final approval decision. After approval, you schedule your closing and sign the new loan documents.

One thing worth knowing: on most refinances of a primary residence, federal law gives you three business days after closing to cancel the loan if you change your mind. This is called the right of rescission, and it is a protection built in specifically for refinances.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Refinancing in Windsor CO

**How much does a refinance cost in Windsor?**
Closing costs typically fall between 2 and 5 percent of the loan amount. On a $380,000 loan, that is roughly $7,600 to $19,000. Some lenders offer no closing cost options, where those costs are rolled into the loan or offset by a slightly higher interest rate.

**How do I know if refinancing is worth it financially?**
The simplest check is a break even calculation. Divide your total closing costs by your monthly savings. The result tells you how many months it takes to break even. If you plan to stay in your Windsor home longer than that, a refinance is likely worth doing.

**Can I refinance if I have not owned the home very long?**
Usually yes, though some loan programs require a waiting period of 6 to 12 months after your original purchase before you can refinance. The rules depend on your loan type.

**Will applying to refinance hurt my credit score?**
It can cause a small, temporary dip. Every loan application triggers a credit inquiry, which may lower your score by a few points for a short time. If you shop with multiple lenders within a window of about 14 to 45 days, most credit scoring models count it as a single inquiry, so comparing options does not multiply the impact.

**What if I have an FHA loan right now?**
If your current mortgage is an FHA loan, which is a government backed loan typically used for lower down payments, you may qualify for an FHA streamline refinance. This program involves less paperwork and often does not require a new appraisal. You generally need at least 6 months of on time payments before you can apply.
