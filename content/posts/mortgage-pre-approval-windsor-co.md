## What Is a Mortgage Pre-Approval?

A pre-approval is a written statement from a lender. It says that, based on a review of your income, credit, and debts, you qualify to borrow up to a certain amount. It is not a final guarantee, but it is a strong, verified number you can count on.

A pre-qualification is different. With a pre-qualification, a lender gives you a rough estimate based on what you tell them, without actually checking your documents or your credit. Pre-qualification is a fine starting point, but pre-approval carries much more weight.

When you make an offer on a home in Windsor, sellers in a competitive market want to see that you have been verified by a lender. A pre-approval letter gives them that confidence.

## Mortgage Pre-Approval in Windsor CO: What You Need to Get Started

Gathering your documents ahead of time makes the pre-approval process move fast. Most lenders will ask for:

Your last two pay stubs. Your W-2s or 1099s from the past two years. Two to three months of bank statements. A government-issued ID. Your Social Security number so the lender can pull your credit report.

If you are self-employed, expect to provide two years of tax returns and a profit and loss statement for the current year. If you have rental income, investment accounts, or other assets, bring statements for those too.

Once you have your documents in order, a pre-approval can often be issued within 24 to 48 hours. Being organized is the fastest path to getting that letter.

## How Your Credit Score Affects Your Pre-Approval

Your credit score plays a big role in what loans you qualify for and what interest rate you get. A higher score usually means better terms and a lower monthly payment.

You do not need perfect credit to get approved. FHA loans, which are backed by the federal government, allow credit scores as low as 580 with a 3.5 percent down payment. Conventional loans generally want a score of 620 or higher. VA loans, available for veterans and active military, have no official minimum score, though most lenders prefer 580 or above. USDA loans, which allow zero down payment in eligible areas, also have flexible credit requirements.

If your score is not where you want it right now, a good mortgage broker can review your situation and tell you the specific steps that would help before you apply.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Mortgage Pre-Approval in Windsor CO

**How long does a pre-approval letter last?**
Most pre-approval letters are valid for 60 to 90 days. If your home search goes longer, your lender can refresh the approval with updated documents. It does not start the whole process over.

**Does getting pre-approved hurt my credit score?**
It can lower your score by a few points temporarily because of the hard credit inquiry. If you apply with multiple lenders within a short window of about 14 to 45 days, the credit bureaus usually count all of those as a single inquiry. The impact is small and fades quickly.

**What is the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?**
Pre-qualification is an estimate based on what you report. Pre-approval is based on verified documents and a real credit check. Sellers in Windsor take pre-approval much more seriously.

**Can I get pre-approved if I am self-employed?**
Yes. You will need to provide two years of tax returns and a current profit and loss statement. Lenders average your income over those two years to determine your qualifying amount. There are loan programs built for people who work for themselves.

**How soon can I get a pre-approval?**
If you have all your documents ready, many lenders can issue a pre-approval letter within 24 to 48 hours. The more organized you are at the start, the faster it happens.
