If you have your eye on a bigger property in Gilmer or somewhere out in Upshur County, you may have heard the term jumbo loan. Jumbo loans in Gilmer TX are home loans that go above the limit set by the federal government for standard mortgages. For most counties in Texas, that limit is around $806,500 in 2026. If the home you want to buy costs more than that, a jumbo loan is what you would use. And yes, they are available right here in East Texas.

## How Jumbo Loans in Gilmer TX Are Different from Standard Loans

Most home loans follow guidelines set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. Those are two government backed companies that buy mortgages from lenders. Their guidelines include a cap on how much you can borrow. When you borrow more than that cap, you step into jumbo loan territory.

Jumbo loans do not have that same cap, so you can borrow what you actually need for a higher priced home. The trade off is that lenders carry more risk with jumbo loans. Because of that, they tend to be more selective about who they approve and what the loan terms look like.

## What You Need to Qualify

Lenders want to feel confident lending a larger amount, so the requirements are a step up from a standard mortgage. Here is what you can generally expect.

A credit score of at least 700 is common for jumbo loans. Many lenders prefer 720 or higher.

Your debt to income ratio should be below 43 percent. That ratio compares your monthly debt payments to your gross monthly income. Lower is better.

You will likely need a larger down payment. Ten percent down is common, and some lenders ask for 20 percent.

Cash reserves matter too. Lenders may want to see that you have several months of mortgage payments sitting in savings after closing. That gives them confidence that you can handle the loan even if something changes in your life.

Every lender sets their own requirements, and those standards can vary quite a bit. That is where working with a mortgage broker helps. A broker can shop multiple lenders and find the one whose standards fit your situation best.

## Rural Properties and Upshur County Considerations

Gilmer sits in the heart of Upshur County, and a lot of properties here are on the larger side. You might be looking at a home on several acres, a farmhouse with outbuildings, or a place that has plenty of room for horses or livestock. Jumbo loans can work on these types of properties, though the details matter.

If you are buying rural land with a home on it, your lender will look at how the property is classified. A home sitting on a few acres usually qualifies the same as a standard residential property. Properties that generate income from farming or livestock may be treated differently by underwriters.

The key is to talk through your specific property early in the process. That way there are no surprises at closing.

## What to Expect on Interest Rates

Jumbo loan rates used to be significantly higher than standard loan rates. Today the gap is much smaller, and sometimes the rates are close to each other. Your rate will depend on your credit score, your down payment, how much you are borrowing, and which lender you work with.

Shopping around matters a lot with jumbo loans. Because these loans are not sold to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac, each lender holds them on their own books and sets their own pricing. One lender might offer a rate that is half a percent lower than another. Over the life of a 30 year loan, that adds up to real money.

## FAQ

**What is the jumbo loan limit in Gilmer TX?**
In Upshur County and most of Texas, the conforming loan limit is around $806,500 in 2026. If your loan amount goes above that number, you would be looking at a jumbo loan.

**Can I get a jumbo loan with 10 percent down?**
Yes. Some lenders offer jumbo loans with 10 percent down. Others require 20 percent. Your credit score and overall financial picture will influence what is available to you.

**Are jumbo loan rates higher than regular loan rates?**
They used to be much higher. Today the difference is smaller. Your rate depends on your credit score, your down payment amount, and which lender you choose.

**Can a jumbo loan be used for a property with land in Upshur County?**
It depends on the property. A home on a few acres typically qualifies just fine. If the land generates farming income or if the acreage is very large, lenders may evaluate it differently. Talk through the details early so you know what to expect.

**Do I need perfect credit to get a jumbo loan?**
No. You need strong credit, which usually means a score of 700 or higher. Some lenders prefer 720 or above. You do not need a perfect score, but a strong credit history will help you get better terms.
