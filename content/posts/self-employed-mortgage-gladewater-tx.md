Getting a self-employed mortgage in Gladewater TX is very possible. The challenge is not your income — it is how traditional lenders read it. If you write off business expenses the way any smart business owner does, your tax return shows less income than you actually earn. Banks use that lower number. That is when things get frustrating.

But there are loan programs built specifically for borrowers like you, and working with the right broker makes a significant difference.

## Why Self-Employment Complicates the Mortgage Process

When you work a W-2 job, a lender can verify your income with one document and two minutes. When you run a business, own a trade, or work as an independent contractor in Gladewater, the verification process looks different.

Lenders want to see that your income is real, consistent, and likely to continue. They use tax returns, profit and loss statements, and sometimes bank statements to piece that picture together. The complication is that tax returns often understate income for business owners because deductions reduce your taxable income — which is exactly the point of running a business correctly, but it works against you on a standard mortgage application.

## Bank Statement Loans for Gladewater TX Business Owners

A bank statement loan is the most popular solution for self-employed buyers who cannot qualify using their tax returns. Instead of looking at what you wrote off, the lender looks at what actually came into your bank account over the past 12 to 24 months and averages out your deposits.

If you run a business in Gladewater or anywhere in Gregg County and your bank account reflects strong, steady cash flow, a bank statement loan can get you qualified when a conventional loan would not. Credit score requirements generally start around 620, and down payments typically range from 10 to 20 percent depending on your overall profile.

## Conventional Loans for Self-Employed Borrowers

If your tax returns do reflect enough qualifying income, a conventional loan is worth looking at. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac allow self-employed borrowers with at least two years of documented self-employment history to qualify, and the rates on conventional loans are usually better than specialty programs.

You will need two years of personal and business tax returns, a year-to-date profit and loss statement, and a debt-to-income ratio that fits within program guidelines. A credit score of 620 or higher is required, and down payments can start as low as three to five percent depending on the loan structure.

## DSCR Loans If You Are Buying an Investment Property

If you are self-employed and looking at rental property in Gladewater or the surrounding East Texas area, a DSCR loan is worth knowing about. These loans qualify you based on the income the property generates, not your personal income. That means your business write-offs do not affect your ability to qualify at all.

DSCR loans are popular with investors who already have significant deductions on their personal returns. You will typically need at least 20 to 25 percent down and a property that cash flows reasonably well.

## Documents to Have Ready

For a bank statement loan: 12 to 24 months of personal or business bank statements, a profit and loss statement from a CPA, and documentation of self-employment such as a business license.

For a conventional or FHA loan: two years of personal tax returns, two years of business tax returns if applicable, a year-to-date profit and loss statement, and recent bank statements.

Getting these together before you apply speeds up the process considerably.

## FAQ: Self-Employed Mortgage in Gladewater TX

**Can I qualify if I have only been self-employed for one year?**

Most programs want two years. Some bank statement loan programs will accept one year if you had prior experience in the same field. Your broker can tell you exactly where you stand based on your specific situation.

**Do my business write-offs hurt my chances?**

On a conventional or FHA loan, yes — because lenders use your taxable income after deductions. On a bank statement loan, no — the lender looks at your actual deposits, not your tax return.

**What credit score do I need?**

FHA loans allow scores as low as 580. Conventional loans generally start at 620. Bank statement loans vary by lender but often require 620 or higher. A stronger score always improves your rate options.

**What if my income is seasonal or varies month to month?**

That is common with self-employment, and lenders account for it. They will average your income over 12 or 24 months to arrive at a stable qualifying number.

**How long does it take to close?**

A typical timeline is 30 to 45 days. Having your documents organized before you apply can move things along faster.
