## What Are Mortgage Rates and Why Do They Matter in Longview TX?

A mortgage rate is the interest your lender charges you for borrowing money to buy a home. Every month, a piece of your payment goes toward the principal (the amount you borrowed) and another piece goes toward interest. The lower your rate, the smaller that interest portion is, which means more of your money builds equity in the home.

In Longview, where the East Texas housing market has stayed steady and affordable compared to many other parts of the state, even a small change in rate makes a real difference. On a $275,000 home with a 5% down payment, dropping your rate by half a percent can save you around $80 a month. Over 30 years, that adds up to nearly $29,000. That is real money, and it is worth taking the time to shop for the best rate you can get.

## What Affects Your Mortgage Rate in Longview TX?

Rates are not one size fits all. Here are the main factors that go into the rate you get quoted.

**Your credit score.** This is probably the single biggest factor. Lenders use it to judge how likely you are to repay the loan on time. A score above 740 usually gets the best available rates. A score below 640 will likely mean a higher rate, or fewer loan options to choose from.

**Your down payment.** Putting more money down reduces the lender's risk. Putting down 20% or more often gets you a better rate and lets you skip private mortgage insurance, which is an extra cost added to your monthly payment.

**The loan type.** Conventional loans, FHA loans, VA loans, and USDA loans all carry slightly different rates. VA loans for veterans and active military often come with some of the lowest rates available. FHA loans are a great fit for buyers with lower credit scores or smaller down payments.

**The loan term.** A 15 year loan typically has a lower rate than a 30 year loan. But the monthly payments are higher. Most buyers in Longview choose the 30 year term for the lower monthly payment and more breathing room in the budget.

**Economic conditions.** Mortgage rates move with inflation, the job market, and decisions by the Federal Reserve. Rates can shift week to week, so staying in touch with a local broker helps you act when the timing is right.

## Fixed vs. Adjustable Mortgage Rates Longview TX

When you shop for a home loan in Longview, you will hear about two main rate structures.

A fixed rate mortgage keeps the same interest rate for the entire life of your loan. Your payment never changes, which makes it easy to plan your budget month after month. Most home buyers in East Texas choose a 30 year fixed rate for this reason.

An adjustable rate mortgage, often called an ARM, starts with a lower rate for a set number of years, usually 5 or 7, and then adjusts periodically based on market conditions after that. An ARM can work well if you plan to sell or refinance before the adjustment period kicks in. But if you stay in the home past that point, your rate and payment could go up.

For most families putting down roots in Gregg County, a fixed rate is the safer and simpler choice.

## How to Get a Lower Mortgage Rate in Longview TX

You have more control over your rate than you might think. Here are some steps that can help.

**Work on your credit score first.** Pay down balances on your credit cards and avoid applying for new credit in the months before you apply for a mortgage. Even a 20 to 30 point improvement in your score can move the needle on your rate.

**Save a larger down payment.** If you can stretch to 10% or 20% down instead of the minimum, your rate will often improve.

**Lock your rate once you have a purchase agreement.** A rate lock protects you if rates climb while you are waiting to close. Locks usually last 30 to 60 days, which covers most standard closings in Longview.

**Consider buying points.** You can pay an upfront fee, called mortgage points, to lower your interest rate permanently. One point equals 1% of the loan amount. This strategy makes sense if you plan to stay in the home for several years and want to reduce your long term cost.

**Work with a local mortgage broker.** A broker shops your loan across multiple lenders at once. That gives you more options than walking into a single bank, and a local broker who knows the Longview and East Texas market can match you with lenders that work well in this area.
