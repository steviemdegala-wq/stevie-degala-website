## What Is a Construction Loan?

A construction loan is a loan that pays for building your home. Instead of getting one big check upfront, the lender releases money in stages as your home is being built. Each stage is called a "draw," and it matches a milestone in construction, like pouring the foundation or framing the walls.

Once your home is finished, the construction loan is paid off. At that point, most people convert it into a regular mortgage, which is a long term home loan. Some lenders offer what is called a "one time close" loan, where you sign once and the loan automatically becomes a mortgage when construction is done. That saves you from going through two separate closings.

## How to Qualify for a Construction Loan

Construction loans have a few more steps than a standard mortgage, but they are not out of reach. Here is what most lenders look for.

A credit score of around 680 or higher. A down payment, often between 10 and 20 percent of the total project cost. A licensed general contractor with a solid plan and a realistic budget. Income that is stable enough to support payments during the build.

The lender will also look at your builder's credentials. They want to know the person doing the work is qualified and insured. If you have a builder in mind, gather their license information and a few references before you apply. Having that paperwork ready speeds things up.

## What to Expect During the Process

Here is a simple breakdown of how a construction loan typically works.

First, you get preapproved. Your lender looks at your income, credit, and overall finances. Second, you finalize your plan and budget with your builder. Third, the lender approves the project and you close on the loan. Fourth, construction begins and the lender releases funds in stages as milestones are met. Fifth, your home is complete and the loan converts to a regular mortgage.

The whole process usually takes six to twelve months from start to finish. Some builds take longer, especially when adding custom features or waiting on materials. Having a good builder and a clear timeline helps everything go smoothly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I build on raw land with a construction loan?**
Yes. Many construction loans let you buy the land and build the home with the same loan. If you already own the land, you may be able to use its value toward your down payment.

**Do I make payments during construction?**
Usually yes, but they are often interest only payments on the money that has been drawn so far. So your payment starts small and grows as more of the loan is used.

**What if my build goes over budget?**
Plan for this ahead of time. Construction costs can climb, especially in today's market. Ask your lender about building a contingency amount into the budget, and talk to your builder about what could change the final cost.

**Is it hard to find a lender for construction loans in Gilmer TX?**
Not if you work with a broker who knows the area. A local mortgage broker can connect you with lenders who are familiar with Upshur County properties and rural builds.

**Can first time buyers get a construction loan?**
Yes! First time buyers can qualify for construction loans. Some programs are specifically designed for buyers who are new to homeownership, so do not count yourself out.
