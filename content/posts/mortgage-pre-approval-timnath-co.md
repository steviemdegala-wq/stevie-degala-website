## What Is a Mortgage Pre-Approval?

A pre-approval is a letter from a lender that says you qualify for a loan up to a certain amount. To get it, you share your income, employment history, assets, and credit information. The lender reviews everything and gives you a written commitment.

This is different from a pre-qualification, which is just a rough estimate based on numbers you self-report. A pre-approval involves real verification. Sellers and their agents know the difference, and they take pre-approvals much more seriously.

## Mortgage Pre-Approval in Timnath CO: What You Will Need

The documents you need for a pre-approval are similar no matter where you are buying, but it helps to have them organized before you reach out to a lender. Here is what most lenders will ask for.

Proof of income. This usually means your last two years of tax returns, your two most recent pay stubs, and W-2s from the same period. If you are self-employed, you will need business returns and possibly a year-to-date profit and loss statement.

Bank and asset statements. Lenders want to see where your down payment is coming from and that you have enough left over after closing. Pull together your last two to three months of statements for any account you plan to use.

Credit information. Your lender will pull your credit as part of the process. You do not need to pull it yourself first, but it is smart to know roughly where you stand. A score above 620 opens most loan programs. Higher scores generally mean better rates.

Employment verification. Your lender will likely contact your employer directly. Having your employer's contact information ready speeds the process up.

## How Long Does Pre-Approval Take?

If you have your documents ready, a pre-approval can move quickly. Many lenders can issue a pre-approval letter within one to three business days once they have everything they need. Some can do it even faster if your situation is straightforward.

The pre-approval letter is typically good for 60 to 90 days. If your home search takes longer than that, your lender will update the letter with fresh income and credit verification.

## What Happens After You Get Pre-Approved?

Once you have your pre-approval letter, you can start shopping with confidence. Here are a few things to keep in mind during that time.

Do not open new credit accounts. Taking on new debt between pre-approval and closing can change your debt to income ratio, which is the percentage of your monthly income that goes toward debt payments. A big change there could affect your loan.

Do not make large deposits you cannot explain. Lenders will review your bank statements again at closing. Unexplained large deposits raise questions. If you receive a gift for your down payment, let your lender know right away so they can document it properly.

Do not change jobs if you can avoid it. Lenders love consistent employment. If a job change is unavoidable, talk to your lender first so they can advise you on how it might affect your file.

Keep saving. Even after you are pre-approved, continuing to add to your savings shows stability and gives you more cushion at closing for things like prepaid insurance or property taxes.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Mortgage Pre-Approval in Timnath CO

**Does getting pre-approved hurt my credit score?**

Your lender will do what is called a hard inquiry when they pull your credit for a pre-approval. This can lower your score by a few points temporarily. If you shop with multiple lenders within a short window, typically 14 to 45 days, the credit bureaus treat it as a single inquiry. So comparing lenders will not hurt you as much as you might fear.

**Can I get pre-approved before I have a specific home in mind?**

Yes, and that is actually the right order. Get pre-approved first so you know your price range. Then start looking at homes.

**What if my credit score is lower than I expected?**

A lower score does not always mean you cannot buy. FHA loans, for example, allow scores as low as 580 with a 3.5 percent down payment. Some programs go even lower. Talk to a mortgage broker who can look at your full picture and find options that fit.

**How is pre-approval different from a loan commitment?**

A pre-approval is based on your finances at the time of application. A loan commitment comes later, after a specific property is identified, the appraisal comes back, and final underwriting is complete. Pre-approval means you are qualified. A commitment means the loan is approved for a specific property.

**Can I use my pre-approval for a new construction home in Timnath?**

Yes. Many buyers in Timnath are purchasing new construction. A pre-approval works the same way. One thing to be aware of is that new construction timelines can run six months to a year or more, so your lender may need to refresh the pre-approval before you close.
