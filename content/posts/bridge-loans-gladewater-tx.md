## What Is a Bridge Loan?

A bridge loan is exactly what it sounds like. It bridges the gap between buying a new home and selling the one you already own. The loan is typically short term, usually six to twelve months. During that time, you use it to cover the down payment or even the full purchase price of your next home.

Once your current home sells, you pay off the bridge loan with the sale proceeds. The process is clean and simple, and it gives you real flexibility in a market where good homes do not stay available for long.

## How Bridge Loans in Gladewater TX Are Structured

Most bridge loans work in one of two ways.

The first option adds the bridge loan on top of your current mortgage. You carry both loans for a short period while your home is listed for sale. The second option pays off your existing mortgage and rolls everything into one combined loan.

Your lender will look at the equity you have in your current home to decide how much you can borrow. Generally, you can access up to 80 percent of the combined value of both properties. You will pay interest on the bridge loan, usually at a slightly higher rate than a traditional mortgage. That is expected because it is a short term product. The goal is to pay it off quickly once your home sells.

## Bridge Loan Costs to Keep in Mind

Bridge loans are short term tools, and they come with costs that are a little higher than a standard home loan. Here is what to expect.

Interest rates on bridge loans are typically one to two percent higher than a regular mortgage rate. You may also pay origination fees and closing costs at the start. Some lenders charge interest only during the loan term, which keeps your monthly payment lower while you wait for your home to sell. When your home closes, the full principal balance is due.

The key is to weigh the cost of the bridge loan against the value of the home you are trying to buy. If locking in a great home at today's price saves you more than the bridge loan costs you, it is often worth it.

## FAQ: Bridge Loans Gladewater TX

**Can I get a bridge loan if I already have a mortgage on my current home?**

Yes. Most bridge loan borrowers still have an existing mortgage. The bridge loan sits alongside that loan until your current home sells and you pay it off.

**How quickly can a bridge loan close?**

Bridge loans often close faster than traditional mortgages. Many lenders can process them in two to three weeks, which matters a lot if you need to act quickly on a property.

**What if my home takes longer to sell than expected?**

Bridge loans are short term, so a longer timeline can be a problem. Talk with your lender upfront about extension options or what happens if your sale takes more time than planned.

**How much equity do I need to qualify?**

Most lenders want to see at least 20 percent equity in your current home. The more equity you have, the more flexible your options will be.

**Can I use a bridge loan to buy rural property in Gladewater?**

Yes. Rural lots and properties in the Upshur County area can work for bridge loan financing as long as the property appraises at a reasonable value and meets the lender's guidelines.
