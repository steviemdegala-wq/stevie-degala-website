## What Mortgage Pre-Approval in Gladewater TX Actually Means

Pre-approval is not the same as just getting a quick estimate. A pre-approval means a lender has actually looked at your finances, verified your income and credit, and committed to lending you a specific amount under certain conditions.

When you hand a seller a pre-approval letter in Gladewater, they know you are not just browsing. They know a real lender has done the homework and you are ready to close. That matters, especially when multiple buyers want the same property.

Do not confuse pre-approval with pre-qualification. Pre-qualification is just a rough guess based on what you say about yourself. Pre-approval is a real review with real documentation. Always go with pre-approval.

## What Documents You Need to Gather

The process is much smoother when you show up prepared. Here is what most lenders will ask for:

**Proof of income.** This means your two most recent pay stubs if you work a salaried or hourly job. If you are self-employed, bring your last two years of federal tax returns plus a year-to-date profit and loss statement.

**Tax returns.** Most lenders want your last two years of federal tax returns, including all schedules. W-2s from those same years help too.

**Bank statements.** Two to three months of statements from your checking, savings, and any investment accounts. Lenders want to see that you have enough for a down payment and closing costs.

**Photo ID and Social Security number.** These are standard for any credit check.

**Employment verification.** Your lender may contact your employer directly, or ask for an offer letter if you recently started a new job.

Getting these together before you call saves a lot of back and forth. Gladewater buyers who come in prepared often get their letters back within one to two business days.

## How Your Credit Score Affects the Process

Your credit score is one of the biggest factors in your pre-approval. Here is a simple breakdown of what to expect:

A score of 740 or higher puts you in the best position. You will qualify for the lowest rates and have the most loan options available.

A score between 620 and 739 still gives you strong options. FHA loans, (loans backed by the Federal Housing Administration), are available starting around 580. Conventional loans, (loans that follow Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac guidelines), typically want at least a 620.

A score below 580 does not mean you are out of options. It may mean a little more prep work before you apply. Things like paying down a credit card or correcting an error on your report can move your score faster than you might think.

If your score needs work, a good mortgage broker can walk you through exactly what to do. There is no shame in taking sixty days to clean things up before applying.

## How Long Pre-Approval Takes and How Long It Lasts

Once you submit your documents, most lenders can issue a pre-approval letter within one to three business days. If your file is straightforward, it can be even faster.

Pre-approval letters are typically good for 60 to 90 days. If you have not found a home by then, your lender will refresh the letter by pulling an updated credit report and confirming your employment is still the same. This is quick and usually takes only a day.

If your financial situation changes during that window, like starting a new job, taking on a new debt, or making a large purchase, let your lender know. Those changes can affect what you qualify for, and it is better to know early.

## FAQ: Mortgage Pre-Approval in Gladewater TX

**Does getting pre-approved hurt my credit score?**

A pre-approval involves a hard credit inquiry, which may lower your score by a few points temporarily. If you apply with multiple lenders within a short window, usually 14 to 45 days, most scoring models count those as a single inquiry. The small dip is worth it for the confidence and buying power you get.

**Can I get pre-approved if I am self-employed?**

Yes. Self-employed buyers do need to provide a little more documentation, mainly two years of tax returns and a profit and loss statement. If your income is consistent and your taxes are filed, the process is very similar to a standard application.

**What if I find a home before I have my pre-approval?**

If you fall in love with a home before you have your letter, contact your lender immediately and let them know. Many lenders can rush a letter in 24 hours if needed. But honestly, starting the pre-approval before you tour homes puts you in a much stronger position.

**Does pre-approval guarantee I will get the loan?**

Pre-approval is a strong commitment but not a guarantee. The final loan approval happens after a home is under contract and goes through underwriting. As long as nothing major changes in your finances before closing, an approval very rarely falls through.

**What is the difference between a mortgage broker and a bank?**
