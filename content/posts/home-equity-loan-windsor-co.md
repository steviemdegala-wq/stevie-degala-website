## What Is a Home Equity Loan?

A home equity loan is a second mortgage. You borrow a lump sum of money and pay it back over a set number of years at a fixed interest rate. The amount you can borrow depends on how much equity you have. Equity is simply the difference between what your home is worth and what you still owe on your mortgage.

For example, if your home is worth $500,000 and you owe $300,000, you have $200,000 in equity. Most lenders let you borrow up to 80 or 85 percent of your home's value minus what you owe. In this example, you might be able to borrow $100,000 to $125,000.

Because the loan is secured by your home, the interest rate is usually much lower than a personal loan or credit card. And because the rate is fixed, your monthly payment stays the same from the first payment to the last.

## How a Home Equity Loan Is Different From a HELOC

You may have heard of a HELOC, which stands for home equity line of credit. Both let you tap your home's value, but they work differently.

With a home equity loan, you get one lump sum and pay it back at the same fixed rate every month. With a HELOC, you get a line of credit you can draw from as needed, and the rate is usually variable. That means your payment can change over time.

A home equity loan is a great fit if you know exactly how much you need and want a predictable payment. A HELOC works better if your needs are spread out or ongoing.

## What You Need to Qualify for a Home Equity Loan in Windsor CO

Lenders look at a few key things when you apply. The main ones are your credit score, your income, and how much equity you have in your home.

Most lenders want to see a credit score of at least 620. A score above 700 will get you better interest rates. You will also need to show that your income is steady and that your total monthly debt payments, including the new loan, stay within a reasonable portion of what you earn. Lenders call this your debt to income ratio.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to get a home equity loan in Windsor CO?**
Most home equity loans close in two to four weeks. An appraisal is usually required to confirm your home's value, which adds a bit of time. Starting the process early helps if you have a deadline in mind.

**Can I use a home equity loan for anything I want?**
Yes. Once the funds are in your account, you can use them however you choose. Renovations, debt payoff, tuition, and large purchases are all common uses. The lender does not restrict how you spend the money.

**Will a home equity loan affect my current mortgage?**
No. Your current mortgage stays exactly as it is. A home equity loan is a separate loan with its own monthly payment. You will simply make two payments each month, one for your first mortgage and one for the new loan.

**What happens if I sell my home before the loan is paid off?**
When you sell, both your first mortgage and your home equity loan are paid off from the sale proceeds. Any remaining equity comes to you at closing.

**Do I need an appraisal to get a home equity loan?**
Usually yes. Lenders need to confirm your home's current value before they finalize the loan amount. Some lenders use automated tools for smaller loans, but a full appraisal is common for larger amounts.
