## What Are Investment Property Loans?

An investment property loan is a mortgage you use to buy a home you do not plan to live in yourself. You might rent it out to long term tenants, use it as a short term vacation rental, or hold it and let it appreciate over time. Lenders treat these loans differently from loans on a primary home, and it helps to understand what to expect.

The main differences you will notice compared to a regular home loan:

Your down payment will be higher. Most lenders require at least 15 to 25 percent down on an investment property. This is because the loan is seen as riskier when you are not living there yourself.

Your interest rate will be a little higher too. Lenders add a small premium to investment property rates to account for that extra risk.

Lenders will look closely at your credit score. Most want to see a score of 680 or higher, and a score above 720 will get you the best pricing.

Your debt to income ratio matters. This is the percentage of your monthly income that goes toward debt payments. Keeping it below 45 percent gives you more room to work with.

## Loan Options for Investment Properties in Windsor CO

There is not a one size fits all loan for investment property buyers. Here are the main options worth knowing about.

**Conventional Investment Property Loans**

This is the most common option. Conventional loans are not backed by the government, and they follow guidelines set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. You can use a conventional loan to buy a single family rental or a property with up to four units. Down payments typically start at 15 percent for a single unit and go up from there for multi-unit properties.

**DSCR Loans (Debt Service Coverage Ratio Loans)**

DSCR loans have become very popular with real estate investors, and for good reason. With a DSCR loan, the lender focuses on whether the rental income from the property is enough to cover the mortgage payment. Your personal income, tax returns, and employment history matter much less. If the numbers on the property work, the loan often works too.

This makes DSCR loans a great fit for self-employed investors, business owners, or anyone whose personal income looks complicated on paper. Windsor's strong rental market makes it easier to meet the DSCR requirements because rents have stayed healthy.

**Conventional Portfolio Loans**

Some lenders hold loans in their own portfolio instead of selling them to the secondary market. These portfolio loans sometimes have more flexible guidelines, which can help if you own multiple properties or have a situation that does not fit the standard mold.

## Investment Property Loans Windsor CO: What Lenders Look At

Beyond the basics, lenders take a close look at a few specific things when you apply for an investment property loan in Windsor.

Your reserves matter a lot. Lenders want to know you have money left over after closing, usually six to twelve months of mortgage payments saved up. This protects everyone if the property sits vacant for a while.

Rental income history helps but is not always required. If you are buying your first rental property, lenders can use a market rent appraisal to estimate what the home should rent for and count a portion of that toward your qualifying income.

The property itself gets scrutinized too. A property that needs major repairs can make approval harder. Conventional investment property loans generally require the home to be in livable condition.

## How to Get Ready to Apply

Getting a head start before you find a property makes the whole process smoother. Here are a few things you can do right now.

Pull your credit report and review it. If you see anything that looks off, start working on it now. A few points on your credit score can mean a real difference in your rate.

Get your finances organized. Investment property lenders will want to see bank statements, tax returns, and information about any other properties you own.

Think about your down payment source. Lenders want to know where your funds are coming from, and they want to see those funds seasoned in your account for at least sixty days.

Talk to a mortgage broker who knows the Northern Colorado market. A local broker can help you compare options from multiple lenders and find the loan that fits your investment strategy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much do I need to put down on an investment property in Windsor CO?**
Most conventional investment property loans require at least 15 percent down for a single family home and 25 percent for a two to four unit property. DSCR loans may have similar requirements depending on the lender.

**Can I use rental income to qualify for an investment property loan?**
Yes, in most cases. Conventional loans allow you to count a percentage of projected or actual rental income. DSCR loans use the property's rental income as the primary qualifying factor.

**What credit score do I need for an investment property loan?**
Most lenders want to see at least a 680. To get the best rates and the most loan options, aim for 720 or higher.

**Are DSCR loans a good fit for Windsor CO investment properties?**
They can be a great fit. Windsor's strong rental demand and steady rents make it easier for a property to meet the cash flow requirements. DSCR loans also work well for investors who are self-employed or who prefer to qualify based on the property rather than personal income.

**Can I buy a short term rental with an investment property loan?**
Yes, many lenders allow short term rentals. Some DSCR lenders will even use short term rental income projections to qualify the loan. Just confirm Windsor's local rules on short term rentals before you commit to that strategy.
