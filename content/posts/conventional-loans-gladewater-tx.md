## What Are Conventional Loans?

A conventional loan is a home loan that is not backed by a government agency like the FHA or VA. Instead, it follows rules set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, which are two large organizations that buy mortgages from lenders. Because these loans are not government insured, lenders look closely at your credit score, your income, and how much you are putting down.

Here is the good news. If you have solid credit and a decent down payment saved up, a conventional loan often gives you better rates and fewer restrictions than government loan programs. That matters when you are buying in Gladewater, where home prices are much more affordable than in big Texas cities, so every fraction of a percent on your interest rate adds up over 30 years.

## Who Qualifies for Conventional Loans in Gladewater TX?

Most lenders want to see a credit score of at least 620 to approve a conventional loan. A score above 700 usually unlocks better rates. You will also need to show steady income for at least two years, which lenders verify through pay stubs, tax returns, and bank statements.

Your debt to income ratio matters too. That is the percentage of your monthly income that goes toward paying debts. Most lenders prefer this number to be 45 percent or below, though some programs allow a little higher.

For the down payment, the minimum is usually 3 percent for first time buyers and 5 percent for repeat buyers. If you put down 20 percent or more, you skip private mortgage insurance entirely, which can save you a meaningful amount every month.

Since Gladewater sits in both Gregg County and Upshur County, that same limit applies here. Most homes in Gladewater fall well below that number, so you are unlikely to bump into any ceiling issues.

If you ever need to borrow more than the conforming limit, that is called a jumbo loan and it comes with its own set of requirements. But for most buyers shopping in Gladewater today, a standard conventional loan handles the job just fine.

## Private Mortgage Insurance and How to Avoid It

If you put down less than 20 percent, your lender will require private mortgage insurance, which is often called PMI. This is a monthly fee added to your payment that protects the lender if you stop making payments. It is not protecting you. It is protecting the bank.

PMI typically costs between 0.5 percent and 1.5 percent of your loan amount each year. On a $200,000 loan, that could be anywhere from $83 to $250 per month. The good news is that PMI is not permanent. Once you build enough equity in your home, typically reaching 20 percent, you can ask your lender to remove it.

One strategy that works well in Gladewater, where many homes are priced under $200,000, is to save a little longer and come in with a larger down payment. Even going from 5 percent to 10 percent can noticeably reduce your PMI costs.

## FAQ: Conventional Loans in Gladewater TX

**Can I use a conventional loan for a fixer upper in Gladewater?**
A standard conventional loan usually requires the home to be in livable condition. If the property needs major repairs, ask about a conventional renovation loan, sometimes called a HomeStyle loan. It lets you roll the cost of repairs into your mortgage.

**Is a conventional loan better than an FHA loan in Gladewater TX?**
It depends on your situation. FHA loans allow lower credit scores and smaller down payments, but they come with mortgage insurance that often sticks around for the life of the loan. Conventional loans let you drop PMI once you reach 20 percent equity. If your credit is strong, conventional often saves you more money over time.

**Can I buy rural land with a home on it using a conventional loan?**
Yes, in many cases. The property needs to be primarily residential and meet appraisal standards. Homes on acreage in Upshur County and Gregg County have qualified for conventional financing. We just review the details together to confirm.

**How long does it take to close a conventional loan in Texas?**
Most conventional loans close in 30 to 45 days from application. Having your documents ready, including tax returns, pay stubs, and bank statements, can help speed things up.

**Do I need to be a first time buyer to use a conventional loan?**
No. Conventional loans are available to first time buyers and repeat buyers alike. Repeat buyers typically need a slightly larger minimum down payment, but the loan is otherwise available to anyone who qualifies.
