## What Is a Home Equity Loan?

A home equity loan is a second loan you take out on top of your existing mortgage. You borrow a lump sum based on the equity in your home. Equity is the difference between what your home is worth and what you still owe.

For example, if your home is worth $200,000 and you owe $120,000, you have $80,000 in equity. Most lenders let you borrow up to 80 to 85 percent of your home's value, minus what you owe. In that example, you could potentially borrow $40,000 to $50,000.

You pay it back in fixed monthly payments over a set term, usually 10 to 30 years. The interest rate is fixed too, so your payment stays the same every month.

## Home Equity Loan vs. HELOC: Which One Fits You?

People often confuse a home equity loan with a HELOC. HELOC stands for home equity line of credit. They are similar but work differently.

A home equity loan gives you all the money upfront. The rate is fixed. Your payment stays the same every month. It's a good fit when you know exactly how much you need.

A HELOC works more like a credit card. You get a line of credit and draw from it as needed over a set period. The rate is usually variable, meaning it can go up or down over time.

If you want a predictable payment and a set amount, a home equity loan tends to be the better choice. If you want flexibility over several years, a HELOC might fit better. Stevie can walk you through both options and help you pick the one that makes sense for your situation.

## How to Qualify for a Home Equity Loan in Gladewater TX

Lenders look at a few key things when you apply.

Your credit score matters. Most lenders want to see a score above 620. A score above 700 will usually get you a better rate.

Your loan to value ratio is important too. That's how much you owe on your home compared to what it's worth. Most lenders want the total debt, including your first mortgage and the new loan, to stay under 80 to 85 percent of the home's value.

Your income and employment history factor in as well. Lenders want to see that you can handle the new monthly payment on top of your existing mortgage.
