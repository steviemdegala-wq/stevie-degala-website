A bridge loan is a short term loan that uses the equity in your current home to fund your next purchase. It lets you buy now and sell later.

## What Is a Bridge Loan?

A bridge loan is a short term loan. Most bridge loans last between six and twelve months. The loan uses the equity you have built up in your current home as collateral. Equity is simply the difference between what your home is worth and what you still owe on it. Your lender advances a portion of that equity to you. You use the money as your down payment on the new home. When your old home sells, you pay off the bridge loan.

## How Bridge Loans Windsor CO Work Step by Step

Here is what the process looks like for most buyers in Windsor.

First, you work with a mortgage broker to get approved for a bridge loan. The lender looks at the equity in your current home and your ability to repay. You typically need at least 20 percent equity in your current home to qualify.

Second, you use the bridge loan funds as your down payment on the new Windsor home. You close on the new property and move in.

Third, your original home goes on the market. Windsor is a hot real estate market, so homes here often sell quickly. When it sells, you use the proceeds to pay off the bridge loan.

The whole process usually takes just a few months, which fits well with how fast Weld County homes move.

## Who Uses Bridge Loans in Windsor CO?

Bridge loans are a great fit for a few types of buyers.

You might want one if you have already found the right home but your current house is not on the market yet. You might also want one if your home is listed but not yet under contract and a great new property just came available.

Move up buyers use bridge loans all the time. A move up buyer is someone selling one home to buy a bigger or nicer one. It is especially common in a fast moving market like Windsor, where waiting too long can mean losing the home you really want.

Investors also use bridge loans when they need to act quickly on a property before arranging longer term financing.

## Pros and Cons of Bridge Loans

Bridge loans come with some real benefits.

The biggest benefit is speed. You do not have to wait to sell before you buy. You can make a clean offer on the new home without a sale contingency. A sale contingency is a condition in your offer that says you can only buy if your current home sells first. Many sellers in Windsor prefer offers without that condition, especially in a competitive market.

There are also some things to keep in mind. Bridge loans typically have higher interest rates than a standard mortgage. You may also carry two loans at once for a period of time. If your current home takes longer to sell than expected, those costs can add up.

That is why working with an experienced mortgage broker matters. A good broker helps you understand the full cost and structures everything so it works for your situation.

## FAQ: Bridge Loans Windsor CO

**What credit score do I need for a bridge loan in Windsor CO?**

Most lenders look for a credit score of 680 or higher. Some programs may allow a lower score depending on your equity and overall financial picture.

**Can I get a bridge loan if my current home is already listed?**

Yes! Being listed does not disqualify you. In fact, it may speed up the process since you are already moving on the sale.

**How much does a bridge loan cost?**

Bridge loans typically have higher interest rates than standard mortgages. You may also pay origination fees. The exact cost depends on the loan amount, your credit, and your equity. Ask your mortgage broker for a full breakdown before you commit.

**How long does it take to get a bridge loan approved?**

Many bridge loans can close in two to three weeks. Your broker will give you a realistic timeline based on your situation.

**Do I need to make payments on the bridge loan while I carry both loans?**

It depends on how the loan is structured. Some bridge loans require monthly interest payments. Others let you defer payments until your old home sells. Talk through the structure with your mortgage broker so you know exactly what to expect.
