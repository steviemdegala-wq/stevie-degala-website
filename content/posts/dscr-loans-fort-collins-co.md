## What Is a DSCR Loan and How Does It Work?

A DSCR loan is a type of mortgage where the lender looks at the rental income the property brings in and compares it to the monthly loan payment. DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. The math is simple. You divide the monthly rent by the total monthly loan payment, which includes principal, interest, taxes, and insurance. If that number is 1.0 or higher, the property pays for itself. Most lenders want to see a ratio of 1.0 to 1.25.

Here is a quick example. If a property rents for $2,200 a month and the total payment is $2,000 a month, your DSCR is 1.10. That is solid. The lender sees the property as a low risk because the rent covers the payment with room to spare.

You do not need to show your W2s or tax returns for this loan. That is what makes it so useful for so many investors.

## What You Need to Qualify

The requirements are straightforward. Most lenders who offer DSCR loans in Fort Collins CO will look at a few things.

Your credit score matters. A score of 680 or higher is a common starting point, though some lenders will work with scores a bit lower if the deal is strong.

Your down payment will typically be 20 to 25 percent of the purchase price. This is an investment property loan, so lenders want to see more commitment from you upfront.

The DSCR ratio needs to hit that 1.0 mark or above. Some lenders will approve a ratio slightly below 1.0, but you may pay a higher rate for that flexibility.

The property itself needs to be a 1 to 4 unit residential rental. That includes single family homes, duplexes, triplexes, and fourplexes.

## FAQ: DSCR Loans in Fort Collins CO

**Can I use a DSCR loan for a property near Colorado State University?**
Yes. DSCR loans work for properties throughout Fort Collins, including areas near CSU. As long as the rental income supports the loan payment, the specific location within the city is not a barrier.

**Do I need to show my tax returns?**
No. That is one of the biggest advantages of a DSCR loan. The lender qualifies the property on rental income, not your personal income or tax history. This is a game changer for investors with complex returns.

**What is a good DSCR ratio?**
A ratio of 1.0 means the rent exactly covers the payment. Most lenders prefer 1.0 to 1.25 or higher. The higher the ratio, the stronger your application looks to the lender.

**Can I use projected rent if the property is vacant?**
Yes, in most cases. A market rent appraisal from a licensed appraiser shows what the property should earn. Many lenders will accept that in place of an actual signed lease.

**How many investment properties can I have and still use a DSCR loan?**
DSCR loans are designed for investors, so most lenders are comfortable with borrowers who already own multiple properties. The focus is on the deal in front of them, not your total portfolio count.
