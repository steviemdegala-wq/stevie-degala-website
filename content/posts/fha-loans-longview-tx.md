## What Is an FHA Loan?

An FHA loan is a home loan insured by the Federal Housing Administration. Because the government backs the loan, lenders can offer more flexible approval standards than they would for a conventional loan, which is a loan with no government backing. That is why FHA loans are often a great fit for first time buyers or anyone who has had some bumps in their credit history along the way.

The FHA does not lend you the money directly. Instead, it insures the loan so that an approved lender, like me, can offer you better terms than you might find otherwise.

## FHA Loan Requirements in Longview TX

Here is what most lenders will look at when you apply for an FHA loan in Longview.

**Down payment.** You only need 3.5% down if your credit score is 580 or above. If your score falls between 500 and 579, you would need 10% down.

**Credit score.** FHA loans accept scores as low as 580 for the minimum down payment. That is lower than most conventional loans require, which makes FHA a real option for buyers who are still building their credit.

**Debt to income ratio.** This means your total monthly debt payments, including your new mortgage, should generally be no more than 43% to 50% of your gross monthly income. Gross income is your income before taxes come out.

**Mortgage insurance.** FHA loans require mortgage insurance, which is a fee that protects the lender if you stop making payments. There is an upfront fee of 1.75% of the loan amount, and there is also a monthly fee added to your mortgage payment.

**Property condition.** The home you buy must meet certain safety and livability standards. An FHA approved appraiser will inspect the property to confirm it qualifies.

## How FHA Compares to Conventional Loans

A conventional loan usually requires a credit score of at least 620 and a down payment anywhere from 3% to 20%. If your credit score is below 620, a conventional loan may not be an available option at all. FHA loans are more forgiving on credit, which is a big reason so many buyers in Longview choose them.

The main trade off is mortgage insurance. With an FHA loan, you pay mortgage insurance for the life of the loan in most cases. With a conventional loan, you can cancel mortgage insurance once you reach 20% equity, which means you own 20% of the home free and clear. If your credit score is strong and you can comfortably put down 5% or more, it is worth comparing both options side by side. If your credit needs some work or your savings are limited right now, FHA is often the smarter first step.

## FAQ

**Do I have to be a first time buyer to get an FHA loan in Longview TX?**
No. FHA loans are open to anyone who meets the program requirements, whether this is your first home or you have owned before. The main rule is that you can only have one FHA loan active at a time.

**Can I use an FHA loan on a home that needs repairs?**
There is a special version called an FHA 203(k) loan that lets you roll the purchase price and renovation costs into one loan. It is a great option if you find a home you love that needs a little work.

**What is the FHA loan limit for Gregg County TX?**
Loan limits change each year, so the best way to get the current number is to connect with me directly. I will give you the exact limit that applies to your situation.

**How long does closing take on an FHA loan?**
Most FHA loans close in 30 to 45 days from the time your offer is accepted. The timeline depends on how quickly you can provide documents and how active the local market is.

**Can I qualify for an FHA loan if I had a foreclosure or bankruptcy in the past?**
Yes, but there is a waiting period. Most lenders require at least three years after a foreclosure and two years after a bankruptcy discharge before you can qualify for a new FHA loan.
