## What Is an FHA 203k Loan?

The FHA 203k loan is a government backed mortgage that combines a home purchase with renovation financing. Instead of buying a house and then scrambling to find extra money for repairs, you wrap everything together. The Federal Housing Administration insures the loan, which means lenders can offer lower down payments and more flexible credit requirements than you would see with a conventional renovation loan.

There are two versions of the loan. The standard 203k covers major renovations like structural work, room additions, and significant repairs. The limited 203k (sometimes called the streamline version) is for smaller projects, up to around $35,000 in renovation costs. Most buyers in Gladewater use the limited version for things like updating a kitchen, replacing flooring, or fixing a roof.

## How an FHA 203k Loan Works in Gladewater TX

Here is a simple breakdown of how the process works. You find a home you like, even one that needs some work. You get a cost estimate from a licensed contractor. Your lender rolls the purchase price and the renovation costs together into one loan amount. After you close, the contractor gets paid in draws as the work gets done. You make one monthly payment while the renovation is underway.

In Gladewater, this opens up a lot of doors. Homes in Gregg County and Upshur County often carry more character than you find in newer developments, but they can also need updating. An FHA 203k loan lets you turn a home with potential into a home you actually love, all without juggling two separate financing arrangements.

## What Can You Use the Money For?

The FHA 203k loan covers a wide range of improvements. Here are some common uses for buyers in East Texas.

Roof repairs or replacement. A lot of older homes in the area need new roofing, and this is one of the most common repairs buyers roll into their loan.

HVAC systems. Replacing a furnace, air conditioner, or ductwork is an eligible expense.

Kitchen and bathroom updates. Cabinets, countertops, plumbing fixtures, and new appliances can all be included.

Flooring and windows. If a home has worn out floors or drafty windows, you can fix that before you even move in.

Accessibility improvements. Ramps, wider doorways, and other accessibility upgrades are also covered.

What you cannot use the money for: luxury upgrades like swimming pools, outdoor kitchens, or high end landscaping. The loan is focused on making a home livable and safe, not adding extras.

## Who Qualifies for an FHA 203k Loan?

The qualification requirements are similar to a standard FHA loan. Here is what most lenders look for.

Credit score. You typically need a score of at least 580 to qualify for the 3.5% down payment option. If your score falls between 500 and 579, you may still qualify with a 10% down payment.

Down payment. You put down as little as 3.5% of the total loan amount, which includes the purchase price plus renovation costs combined.

Debt to income ratio. This is the percentage of your gross monthly income that goes toward debt payments. Lenders generally want that number to stay below 43%.

Property requirements. The home must be your primary residence. You cannot use an FHA 203k loan on an investment property or a vacation home.

Employment and income. You need to show steady income and a reasonable employment history, usually two years.

If you work at Eastman Chemical or any of the other major employers in the Gladewater area, your income documentation is usually straightforward. Self employed buyers can still qualify, but you will need two years of tax returns to show your lender.

## FAQ

**Can I use an FHA 203k loan to buy a house in Gladewater and fix it up at the same time?**

Yes! That is exactly what the loan is designed for. You buy the home and finance the renovations together in one single mortgage, so there is no second loan to manage.

**How much can I borrow with the limited 203k?**

The limited 203k covers renovation costs up to around $35,000. For larger projects, the standard 203k allows higher amounts, subject to FHA loan limits for Gregg County and Upshur County.

**Do I have to use a specific contractor?**

Your contractor must be licensed and approved by your lender. You cannot do the work yourself on a 203k loan. A licensed professional needs to complete and document everything.

**How long does the renovation have to take?**

For the limited 203k, the work typically needs to be completed within six months of closing. Your lender will walk you through the timeline at the start.

**Is an FHA 203k loan harder to get than a regular FHA loan?**

It is a little more involved because you are coordinating with both a contractor and a lender at the same time. But the credit and income requirements are similar to a standard FHA loan, and a good mortgage broker can guide you through every step.
