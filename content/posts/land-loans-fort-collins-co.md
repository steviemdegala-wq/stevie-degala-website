## What Are Land Loans in Fort Collins CO?

A land loan is financing you use to purchase a vacant parcel. You are not buying a finished home. You are buying the ground itself, and you plan to build on it later.

In Fort Collins and the surrounding Larimer County area, you will run into two main types.

Raw land loans cover undeveloped parcels with no utilities, no water connection, and no road access in place yet. These carry more risk for lenders, which means higher down payments and higher interest rates.

Improved land loans cover lots that already have water, sewer, electricity, and paved road access available. Most planned developments on the edges of Fort Collins fall into this category. Improved lots are easier to finance and come with better terms.

Understanding which kind of lot you are looking at makes a big difference when you start talking to a lender.

## How a Land Loan Works

Land loans are short term products. Most run two to five years. The idea is that you lock in your lot now, then move into a construction loan when you are ready to build. Once your home is finished, the construction loan converts to a permanent mortgage.

Some lenders offer a product that wraps all three steps into one loan. This is called a construction to permanent loan. One application, one closing, one set of closing costs. It covers the land purchase, the build, and your final mortgage in one package. If you know you want to build soon, this combined option is worth asking about.

If you want more flexibility and are not quite ready to build, a standalone land loan lets you secure your lot now and revisit your construction timeline later.

## What Lenders Look at When You Apply

Land loans get more scrutiny than a standard home purchase. Here is what lenders focus on.

Your credit score matters. Most lenders want to see at least 680. A stronger score helps you qualify for better rates and terms.

Your down payment will be larger than you might expect from a typical home loan. Plan for 20 to 35 percent of the purchase price. Improved lots are usually at the lower end of that range. Raw land sits at the higher end.

The lot itself gets evaluated. Lenders want to know if it is zoned for residential use, whether utilities are accessible, and whether there is road access. A parcel in a planned community near Fort Collins generally checks all of those boxes.

Your building plans help your case. Lenders are more comfortable when they can see you have a real timeline. A preliminary design, a builder reservation, or a general construction schedule all strengthen your application.

Your income and debt load still apply. Lenders will calculate your debt to income ratio, which is the share of your monthly income that goes toward debt payments. Keeping that number healthy is important for approval.

## Steps to Get Started With a Land Loan in Fort Collins

Here is a simple path to follow.

First, talk to a mortgage broker before you start shopping for lots. Knowing your budget and which loan products are available to you prevents the process from stalling once you find a parcel you love.

Second, figure out whether the land is raw or improved. This determines which programs are available and what down payment to plan for.

Fifth, apply and get your approval in place. Once you know your numbers and your options, you can move forward with confidence when the right lot comes up.

## FAQ: Land Loans Fort Collins CO

**Can I use a regular home mortgage to buy land in Fort Collins?**
No. Standard purchase mortgages are for properties with a completed home already on them. If you are buying a vacant lot, you need a land loan or a lot loan. A mortgage broker can help you find the right program.

**How much do I need to put down for a land loan?**
Plan for 20 to 35 percent of the purchase price. Improved lots with utilities and road access usually require less. Raw undeveloped land sits at the higher end of that range. Your credit profile affects the final number too.

**Do I need a builder lined up before I apply?**
Not always, but having a builder and a general timeline makes your application much stronger. Lenders want to see that you have a realistic plan to develop the property within the loan term.

**What is a construction to permanent loan?**
It is a single loan product that covers the land purchase, the home build, and your permanent mortgage all in one. You close once, pay one set of closing costs, and convert to a regular mortgage when construction is complete. Many Fort Collins buyers who are ready to build soon use this option.

**Are there any grants or programs for land buyers in Fort Collins or Larimer County?**
Down payment assistance programs in Colorado are generally designed for home purchases rather than raw land. However, if you are exploring new construction in eligible areas, there may be programs worth asking about. Your mortgage broker can look at what is currently available based on your situation.
