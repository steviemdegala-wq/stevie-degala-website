## What Is a Home Equity Loan?

A home equity loan lets you borrow against the value you have built up in your home. Here is how it works. Say your Fort Collins home is worth $500,000 and you owe $300,000 on your mortgage. You have $200,000 in equity, which is just the difference between what your home is worth and what you still owe. A lender will let you borrow a portion of that equity, often up to 80 or 85 percent of your home's value minus what you owe.

You get the money as a lump sum, pay it back in fixed monthly payments over a set term, and your interest rate stays the same the whole time. That makes it easy to budget because you know exactly what you owe each month.

## Home Equity Loan vs. HELOC: What Is the Difference?

You will hear two terms come up a lot when you start researching this. A home equity loan gives you one lump sum at a fixed rate, which is great if you know exactly how much you need. A HELOC, which stands for home equity line of credit, works more like a credit card. You get a credit limit and can draw from it as needed over a set draw period.

The right choice depends on your situation. If you have a specific project with a known cost, a home equity loan often makes more sense. If you have ongoing expenses or want flexibility, a HELOC may be the better fit. A local mortgage broker familiar with Fort Collins can walk you through both options side by side.

## What Do You Need to Qualify for a Home Equity Loan in Fort Collins CO?

Lenders look at a few key factors when you apply.

Your credit score matters. Most lenders want to see a score of at least 620, and better scores typically get you lower interest rates.

Your debt to income ratio also plays a role. This is just a comparison of how much you owe each month versus how much you earn. Lenders want to see that you have enough room in your budget to take on the new payment.

And of course, you need enough equity in your home. Most lenders allow you to borrow up to 80 percent of your home's value when you combine the home equity loan with your existing mortgage balance.

## FAQ: Home Equity Loans in Fort Collins CO

**Can I get a home equity loan if I already have a regular mortgage?**
Yes! A home equity loan is a second loan on top of your existing mortgage. You keep making your regular mortgage payment and add the home equity loan payment to your monthly budget.

**How long does it take to get a home equity loan?**
The process typically takes two to four weeks from application to closing, depending on the lender and how quickly you can provide your documents.

**Does getting a home equity loan affect my first mortgage?**
No. Your first mortgage stays exactly as it is. The home equity loan is a separate loan secured by your home.

**Is home equity loan interest tax deductible?**
The interest may be deductible if you use the loan to buy, build, or substantially improve your home. Talk to a tax professional for advice based on your specific situation.

**What if home values in Fort Collins drop after I take out the loan?**
If values fall significantly, you could owe more than your home is worth. Fort Collins has shown strong long term price stability thanks to Colorado State University, its Front Range location, and consistent demand, but it is worth thinking through before you borrow.
