## Types of Investment Property Loans Available in Longview TX

There is more than one way to finance an investment property in East Texas. Here is a plain language look at what most Longview investors use and when each one makes the most sense.

**Conventional investment property loans** follow Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines. You typically need at least 15 to 25 percent down, a good credit score, and verifiable income from a job or other documented source. These work well if you have a regular job and clean tax returns.

**DSCR loans** let the rental property qualify for the loan on its own income. The lender checks whether the rent covers the monthly payment rather than reviewing your personal income in detail. This is a powerful tool for anyone who is self employed or has income that does not look simple on paper.

**Fix and flip loans** are short term loans that cover the purchase price and renovation costs of a property you plan to resell. They typically run six to eighteen months and fund much faster than conventional loans.

**Hard money loans** come from private lenders and are similar to fix and flip loans. They close even faster but carry higher rates, which makes them best for investors who need speed above all else.

## How DSCR Loans Work for East Texas Investment Properties

DSCR loans are one of the most popular tools for real estate investors in the Longview area right now. DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio, which is just a way of comparing the property's income to its expenses.

The math works like this. If a property rents for $1,500 per month and the loan payment is $1,200 per month, the DSCR is 1.25. Most lenders want to see a ratio of at least 1.0 to 1.25, meaning the rent covers the payment with a little cushion.

What makes this so useful is that your personal income barely matters. The property's rental income carries the approval. You can own several rental properties and still qualify for another one as long as the numbers work on the new property.

DSCR loans typically ask for a down payment of 20 to 25 percent, a credit score of 640 or higher, and a property that can generate enough rent to cover the monthly payment. The approval process involves less paperwork than a conventional loan, and closings tend to happen faster.

For Longview investors, a solid rent estimate from a local property manager before you apply will go a long way toward a smooth approval.

## Conventional Investment Property Financing in Gregg County

Conventional loans are still a great option if you have steady income and clean tax returns. Fannie Mae allows you to finance up to ten investment properties at once if you meet the program requirements. The main hurdle is the down payment, since investment properties require more equity up front than primary residences.

In Gregg County, most conventional investment property loans require at least 15 percent down for a single family rental and 25 percent for a two to four unit property. Interest rates run a bit higher than what you would see on a primary home, but the combination of rental income and East Texas appreciation has worked well for investors who take a patient approach.

If you have good credit and a verifiable income history, a conventional loan often gives you the best long term rate. It takes a little more time to process than a DSCR loan, but it can save you money over the life of the investment.

## Fix and Flip Loans for Longview Properties

East Texas has a lot of older homes that make great candidates for a buy, renovate, and resell strategy. You find a property below market value, put money into it, and sell it for a profit. Fix and flip loans are built for exactly this kind of project.

These loans often close in two weeks or less, which matters a lot when you are competing for an undervalued property and need to act quickly. The lender releases funds in stages as you complete renovation milestones, so you are not carrying the full cost upfront.

Longview neighborhoods near downtown, older subdivisions close to good schools, and areas with easy access to the Gregg County amenities tend to perform well for flippers. Renovated homes near parks and established retail corridors tend to attract buyers quickly once they hit the market.

## What Lenders Look for on an Investment Property Loan Application

Whether you are applying for a DSCR loan, a conventional loan, or a fix and flip loan, lenders focus on a few key areas.

Your credit score carries a lot of weight. A score above 700 opens up the best rates. Scores between 640 and 699 still qualify for most programs, but you will pay a little more.

Your down payment tells the lender how serious you are. The more you put down, the better your rate and terms will be in most cases.

Your cash reserves matter too. Lenders want to see that you have money set aside beyond the down payment. Three to six months of loan payments sitting in savings is a common requirement on investment property loans.

For DSCR loans, the property's cash flow is what closes the deal more than anything else. A conservative rent estimate backed by local market data will serve you better than an optimistic projection you cannot support.

## FAQ: Investment Property Loans in Longview TX

**Can I use a DSCR loan if I am self employed?**

Yes, absolutely. DSCR loans are one of the best tools available for self employed borrowers. The approval focuses on the rental income the property will generate rather than your personal tax returns. As long as the rent covers the loan payment, your employment situation is a much smaller factor.

**How much do I need to put down on an investment property in Longview?**

Most investment property loans require 15 to 25 percent down depending on the loan type and the property. DSCR loans typically ask for 20 to 25 percent. Single family conventional loans can sometimes go as low as 15 percent if your credit score and income are strong.

**Can I buy a small multifamily property in Gregg County with an investment loan?**

Yes. Two to four unit properties qualify for most investment loan programs, including conventional financing and DSCR loans. Properties with five or more units move into commercial territory and use a different type of financing.

**Does having other mortgages make it harder to get a new investment loan?**

Not necessarily. DSCR loans look at each property individually, so having other mortgages does not automatically disqualify you. Conventional loans have debt to income requirements that can tighten things up, but Fannie Mae programs allow up to ten financed properties for qualifying borrowers.

**What is the difference between a fix and flip loan and a new construction loan?**

A fix and flip loan is for purchasing and renovating an existing property you plan to sell. A new construction loan is for building a home from the ground up on a lot. Both are short term products, but they work differently and serve different project types.
