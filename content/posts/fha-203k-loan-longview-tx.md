## What Is an FHA 203k Loan?

An FHA 203k loan is a mortgage backed by the government that bundles the purchase price of a home and the cost of repairs or upgrades into one loan. The Federal Housing Administration insures it, which means lenders can offer it with a lower down payment than most renovation loan options. You can put down as little as 3.5% if your credit score is 580 or higher. If your score is between 500 and 579, you may still qualify with a 10% down payment.

There are two versions of this loan. The Standard 203k covers major renovations like structural repairs, room additions, and big plumbing or electrical overhauls. The Limited 203k, sometimes called the Streamline version, covers smaller cosmetic projects up to around $35,000. That could mean new flooring, updated bathrooms, fresh paint, or a new roof.

## What Kinds of Projects Can You Finance?

With a Limited 203k, you can finance things like kitchen updates, bathroom remodels, new windows, insulation, flooring, and roofing. These are the kinds of repairs that make an older home feel new again without being a full gut renovation.

With a Standard 203k, you can go bigger. Structural repairs, room additions, complete kitchen overhauls, foundation work, and septic system updates are all possible. If you find a home near downtown Longview or in one of the older East Texas neighborhoods that needs significant work, this version of the loan might be the right fit.

The main rule is that the work must start within 30 days of closing and finish within six months. The funds are held in an escrow account and released to the contractor as work is completed. You will need to use a licensed contractor for all repairs.

## How the FHA 203k Loan Process Works

First, you apply and get approved for an FHA 203k loan. Your lender looks at your credit, income, and debts. If you are using the Standard version, you also work with a consultant approved by HUD, the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. That consultant helps scope out the project and estimate costs.

Once you find a home and make an offer, you submit a repair plan along with the offer. After closing, the contractor gets to work. The lender sends payments from the escrow account as milestones are hit. When the work is done, your home gets a final inspection and the last funds are released.

It takes a bit more coordination than a regular home purchase, but the payoff is a home ready to move into that you financed all at once.

## What Credit Score Do You Need?

FHA loans are more flexible with credit than conventional loans. For an FHA 203k loan in Longview TX, most lenders want to see a credit score of at least 580 to qualify for the 3.5% down payment option. Scores between 500 and 579 may still qualify but require more down.

Your income and monthly debt payments matter too. FHA looks at how much of your monthly income goes toward debt. This is called your debt to income ratio. FHA generally allows this to go up to 43%, and sometimes higher with other strong factors in your favor.

## FAQ: FHA 203k Loans in Longview TX

**Can I use an FHA 203k loan to renovate the home I already own?**

Yes! If you own your home and want to renovate it, you can refinance into an FHA 203k loan. You roll the renovation costs into a new mortgage.

**Is there a limit to how much I can borrow?**

Yes. FHA loan limits in Gregg County apply to 203k loans too. The limits are set by HUD and change each year. Check the current limit before you plan your budget so you know what to expect.

**Can I live in the home during renovations?**

With a Limited 203k, most borrowers can live there during the work. For a Standard 203k with major repairs, it depends on the scope. Your lender and HUD consultant can walk you through what to expect before you close.

**What if the renovation costs more than expected?**

There is a contingency reserve built into most 203k loans to handle surprises. It is usually 10% to 20% of the total repair costs. If you do not use it, that money goes toward your loan principal.

**Does this loan work for homes near Tyler or other parts of East Texas?**

An FHA 203k loan applies to your primary residence in eligible areas. Longview and surrounding Gregg County communities qualify. If you are shopping in neighboring areas of East Texas, ask your lender to confirm eligibility for the specific address.
