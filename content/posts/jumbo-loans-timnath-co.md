## What Is a Jumbo Loan and How Does It Work?

A jumbo loan is simply a home loan that is larger than the conforming loan limit for your area. The conforming limit is the maximum amount that Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac will purchase from lenders. When your home price pushes that number up, you need a jumbo loan instead.

In Northern Colorado, including Timnath and the surrounding Larimer County area, home values have climbed steadily because of strong job growth, the pull of Colorado State University in nearby Fort Collins, and the overall desirability of the Front Range lifestyle. That means more buyers are running into jumbo territory, even if they did not expect to.

Here is what makes a jumbo loan different from a regular mortgage. Lenders carry more risk because the government will not buy these larger loans. So they set their own requirements, which are usually a bit stricter than standard guidelines. You will need a strong credit score, proof of steady income, and more cash in the bank.

## What You Need to Qualify for Jumbo Loans in Timnath CO

Qualifying for a jumbo loan is different from qualifying for a regular loan. Here is what lenders typically look for.

**Credit Score.** Most jumbo lenders want to see a score of 700 or higher. Some will go a bit lower, but a stronger score gives you more options and better rates.

**Down Payment.** Expect to put down at least 10 to 20 percent. Some programs allow as little as 5 to 10 percent depending on the lender and your credit profile.

**Income Documentation.** You will need to show your income clearly. That means W2 forms, tax returns, and recent pay stubs. If you are self employed, your lender will likely ask for two years of business returns as well.

**Cash Reserves.** Lenders want to see that you have money left in the bank after you close. Six to twelve months of mortgage payments in savings is a common requirement for jumbo loans.

**Debt to Income Ratio.** This is the percentage of your monthly income that goes toward debt payments. Lenders usually want this number under 43 percent, and sometimes lower for larger loan amounts.

## Jumbo Loans vs. Conforming Loans: Which One Do You Need?

Here is a simple way to think about it. A conforming loan follows the rules set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. These loans are easier to get approved for and sometimes carry slightly lower rates because lenders can sell them in the secondary market.

A jumbo loan does not follow those rules. Lenders keep these loans on their own books, which means they set their own terms. Rates can be very competitive, but the requirements are stricter.

If your purchase price puts your loan amount above the conforming limit, you need a jumbo loan. Your lender can tell you the exact limit for Larimer County so you know which type of loan applies to your situation.

## FAQ: Jumbo Loans in Timnath CO

**How much do I need to put down on a jumbo loan?**

Most jumbo loans require at least 10 percent down, and 20 percent is common. Some programs allow as little as 5 percent depending on your credit score and the lender you work with.

**Are jumbo loan rates higher than regular mortgage rates?**

Not always. Jumbo loan rates can actually be very competitive with conforming loan rates. It depends on the lender and the current market. Shopping with a broker gives you the best chance at a great rate.

**What credit score do I need for a jumbo loan in Timnath?**

Most lenders want a credit score of at least 700. A score of 720 or higher gives you the most options and the best terms.

**How much cash do I need in the bank after closing?**

Most jumbo lenders want to see six to twelve months of mortgage payments in reserves. That is money you have saved and are not using for the down payment.

**Can I get a jumbo loan if I am self employed?**

Yes. Self employed borrowers can qualify for jumbo loans. You will need two years of personal tax returns and possibly two years of business returns as well. Your income is calculated based on what you actually report, so strong documentation matters.
