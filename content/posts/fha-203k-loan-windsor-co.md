## What Is an FHA 203k Loan?

An FHA 203k loan is a renovation mortgage backed by the Federal Housing Administration. With a standard home loan, you get money to buy the house. With a 203k loan, you get money to buy the house AND money to fix it up, all wrapped into one loan. The renovation funds go into an escrow account, which is a holding account managed by your lender, and get released to your contractor as the work gets done.

This program was designed for buyers who want to purchase a home that needs repairs or updates but do not have extra cash sitting around after the down payment. It opens the door to homes you might have written off as too much work.

## Standard 203k vs. Limited 203k: Which One Fits?

There are two versions of the FHA 203k loan, and they serve different needs.

The Limited 203k, sometimes called the Streamline 203k, is for smaller projects. You can finance up to $35,000 in repairs. This version works well for cosmetic updates like new floors, fresh paint, updated appliances, minor roofing work, or new windows. It is simpler to close and does not require as much documentation.

The Standard 203k is for bigger projects. If the home needs structural repairs, a room addition, a full kitchen gut, or major plumbing work, you would use the Standard version. It requires a HUD approved consultant, which is a third party expert who reviews the work plan and helps protect both you and the lender throughout the process.

Most buyers in Windsor use the Limited version for move in ready updates. The Standard version is worth considering if you are truly buying a fixer upper.

## What Work Can You Finance With an FHA 203k Loan?

The program covers a wide range of improvements. Here are some of the most common ones.

You can finance new roofing, updated plumbing or electrical systems, new HVAC units, water heaters, insulation, flooring, and windows. Kitchen and bathroom remodels are also eligible. If the home needs accessibility modifications, those qualify too.

What you cannot do is use the funds for pure luxury upgrades. A hot tub or outdoor pool would not qualify. The goal of the program is to make the home safe, livable, and efficient, not to add extras that go beyond basic habitability.

## How to Qualify for an FHA 203k Loan in Windsor CO

The FHA 203k loan has the same general requirements as a standard FHA loan.

You need a credit score of at least 580 to put down just 3.5 percent. If your score is lower, some lenders will still work with you but may require a larger down payment.

Your debt to income ratio, which is the percentage of your monthly income that goes toward all debt payments combined, should ideally stay below 43 percent. FHA guidelines can be flexible here if you have strong compensating factors like solid cash reserves or a larger down payment.

The home must be your primary residence. You cannot use a 203k loan on an investment property or a vacation home.

After renovation, the property must meet FHA minimum property standards. Your lender will order an appraisal that reflects the value of the home once the work is complete, not just the value as purchased.

## FAQ

**Can I use an FHA 203k loan on a new build in Windsor?**

No. The FHA 203k program is designed for existing homes that need renovation, not new construction. If you want to buy a new build in Windsor, a construction loan or new construction purchase mortgage would be a better fit.

**How long does an FHA 203k loan take to close?**

It typically takes 45 to 60 days, which is longer than a standard purchase. The extra time is needed to coordinate contractor bids and complete the renovation appraisal.

**Can I do the renovation work myself?**

Generally no. FHA requires the work to be completed by licensed contractors. You can manage the project and choose your own contractors, but you cannot pay yourself as the labor.

**What is the loan limit for an FHA 203k loan in Weld County?**

FHA loan limits in Weld County follow the standard FHA limits for the area. These limits adjust each year, so confirm the current figures with your lender before you start shopping.

**Is mortgage insurance required?**

Yes. Like all FHA loans, the 203k program includes both an upfront mortgage insurance premium and an annual premium paid monthly. These costs are built into your loan and reflected in your monthly payment.
