## What Is a USDA Loan and How Does It Work?

A USDA loan is a mortgage backed by the U.S. Department of Agriculture. Despite the name, it has nothing to do with farming. The program was designed to help people buy homes in rural and suburban communities where population density is lower. Gladewater and much of the surrounding area in Gregg County and Upshur County qualify for this program.

Because the federal government backs the loan, lenders take on less risk. That lets them offer buyers better terms, including no down payment requirement and competitive interest rates. Instead of mortgage insurance, USDA loans charge a small upfront guarantee fee and an annual fee, but both of those are typically lower than the cost of private mortgage insurance on a conventional loan.

## Who Qualifies for a USDA Loan in Gladewater?

USDA loans have two main requirements beyond the property location check. First, your income must fall at or below the program's limit for your household size and county. For most buyers in Gregg County and Upshur County, the limit is generous enough to include middle income households. Families with two incomes, kids, or other dependents often qualify even when they expect they earn too much.

Second, you need a credit score that most lenders accept around 640 or higher for automated approval, though some flexibility exists below that. You also need to show stable employment and a debt load that is manageable relative to your income.

First time buyers are welcome, but so are repeat buyers. This is not limited to people purchasing their first home. The main rule is that you cannot own another adequate home at the time of closing.

## USDA Loans vs. Other Options in Gladewater

Here is how USDA compares to the other common loan types in plain terms.

FHA loans require at least 3.5 percent down and charge mortgage insurance for the life of the loan in many cases. They are great for buyers who do not qualify for USDA or need a lower credit score threshold.

Conventional loans typically require 3 to 20 percent down depending on the situation, and they require mortgage insurance until you reach 20 percent equity if you put less than 20 percent down.

VA loans also offer zero down and no mortgage insurance, but you need to have served in the military or be an eligible surviving spouse.

USDA fills the gap for buyers who did not serve in the military and cannot put money down but do live in an eligible rural or suburban area. If you match that description, USDA is almost always the strongest option.

## FAQ: USDA Loans in Gladewater TX

**Do I have to be a first time buyer to use a USDA loan?**
No. USDA loans are open to first time and repeat buyers. The requirement is that you do not currently own an adequate home, not that you have never owned one.

**How long does a USDA loan take to close?**
USDA loans typically take 30 to 45 days, similar to most other loan types. In some cases it can be a few days longer if the file needs additional review through the USDA office, but most lenders have streamlined the process significantly.

**Can I use a USDA loan to buy a home with land?**
Yes, in many cases. Homes with acreage in rural parts of Upshur County or Gregg County can qualify, though the land itself must be typical for the area rather than commercial in nature. A lender familiar with USDA guidelines can help you assess a specific property.

**What is the USDA guarantee fee?**
The upfront guarantee fee is currently 1 percent of the loan amount, and there is an annual fee of 0.35 percent. These fees are what replace private mortgage insurance in the USDA program, and they are typically lower than PMI costs on a comparable conventional loan.
