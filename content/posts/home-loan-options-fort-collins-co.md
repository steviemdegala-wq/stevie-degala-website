## What Is a Home Loan and How Does It Work?

A home loan, also called a mortgage, is money you borrow from a lender to buy a house. You pay it back over time with interest. The rate and loan term affect how much you pay each month.

Most home loans in Fort Collins carry a 30 year term. That means you make monthly payments for 30 years until the balance is paid off. Some buyers choose a 15 year loan to pay it off faster and save money on total interest paid. The right choice depends on your monthly budget and your longer term goals.

When you apply, your lender looks at a few key things. Those are your credit score, your income, your debts, and how much you have saved for a down payment. A higher credit score usually earns you a lower interest rate.

## Types of Home Loans Available in Fort Collins CO

Not every loan works the same way. Here is a plain look at the main types you will come across.

**Conventional loans** are the most common. They are not backed by the government. You typically need a credit score of at least 620 and a down payment anywhere from 3% to 20%. If you put down less than 20%, you will pay PMI. PMI stands for private mortgage insurance. It is a small monthly fee that protects the lender if you stop making payments. Once you have enough equity in the home, you can usually cancel it.

**FHA loans** are backed by the Federal Housing Administration. They allow a credit score as low as 580 with a 3.5% down payment. FHA loans are popular with first time buyers in Fort Collins because they are easier to qualify for and more forgiving of a shorter credit history.

**VA loans** are for veterans, active duty service members, and eligible surviving spouses. They require no down payment and no PMI. If you served in the military and are shopping for homes in Larimer County, a VA loan could save you a significant amount upfront.

**USDA loans** are designed for homes in rural and partly rural areas. Some parts of Larimer County qualify, so it is worth asking your loan officer whether a property you are considering is eligible. No down payment is required with a USDA loan.

**Jumbo loans** are for homes that exceed the standard conforming loan limits set each year by federal regulators. Fort Collins has seen home prices climb with the Front Range growth wave, so jumbo loans come up more often here than in many other Colorado markets.

## How to Get Ready Before You Apply

Getting your finances in order before you apply takes a little time but pays off with a smoother process and often a better interest rate.

Start with your credit report. You can get a free copy from each of the three major credit bureaus once a year. Look for any errors and dispute anything that does not look right. Even small mistakes can drag your score down.

Pay down revolving debt where you can. Credit cards count as revolving debt. The amount you owe compared to your credit limit is called your credit utilization ratio. Keeping that number below 30% helps your score meaningfully.

Save for both your down payment and your closing costs. Closing costs are fees you pay when you finalize the loan. They typically run between 2% and 5% of the loan amount. They cover things like the appraisal, title insurance, and loan origination fees. Some loan programs allow the seller to cover part of your closing costs, so ask your broker about that option.

Avoid big financial moves right before or during the loan process. Switching jobs, buying a vehicle, or opening new credit accounts can all affect your approval or change the loan terms you qualify for.

## FAQ: Home Loans in Fort Collins CO

**What credit score do I need to buy a home in Fort Collins?**
It depends on the loan type. FHA loans accept scores as low as 580. Conventional loans typically want at least 620. A higher score usually earns you a lower rate and better terms.

**How much do I need for a down payment?**
Anywhere from zero to 20% depending on the loan. VA and USDA loans require no down payment. FHA loans need 3.5%. Conventional loans can go as low as 3% for buyers who qualify.

**What are closing costs and how much should I expect to pay?**
Closing costs typically run 2% to 5% of the loan amount. Your lender gives you a Loan Estimate early in the process with an itemized breakdown of what you will owe at closing.

**How long does the loan approval process take in Fort Collins?**
Preapproval usually takes one to three business days once you submit your documents. Full approval and closing after you go under contract on a home typically takes 20 to 45 days.

**Can I buy a home in Fort Collins if I am self employed?**
Yes. Self employed buyers typically need to show two years of tax returns along with business financials. Your mortgage broker can walk you through the full documentation list before you apply so there are no surprises.
