## What Is a DSCR Loan?

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. It is a number that compares the income a rental property brings in to the monthly mortgage payment the property carries. The higher the number, the better.

Here is the math. If a property rents for $1,400 per month and the total mortgage payment, including principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and any HOA fees, comes to $1,100 per month, the DSCR is 1.27. A ratio above 1.0 means the rent covers the payment. Most lenders want to see a DSCR of at least 1.0 to 1.25 before approving a loan.

Some lenders will work with a DSCR below 1.0 if you have strong credit or other compensating factors. Your mortgage broker can help you find lenders who match your situation.

## Who Benefits Most from DSCR Loans in Gladewater TX

DSCR loans are not the right fit for everyone, but they work very well for a specific group of borrowers.

Self-employed investors often have income that looks much lower on paper than it really is. That is because legitimate business deductions reduce what shows up on tax returns. A DSCR loan does not care about your tax returns. It looks at the property.

Portfolio builders run into trouble with traditional loans when lenders calculate a debt to income ratio across multiple properties. DSCR loans sidestep that calculation entirely. The property either pencils out or it does not, and lenders look at each deal on its own terms.

Investors with non-traditional income, like real estate agents, consultants, or those living off investments, often find that standard W2 based underwriting does not reflect their true financial picture. DSCR loans skip that step.

## What You Need to Qualify for a DSCR Loan

Credit score matters even though income documentation does not. Most DSCR lenders want a minimum score of 620. You will see better rates at 680 and above. If your score needs work, it is worth taking a few months to improve it before applying.

Down payment is typically 20 to 25 percent for a single family rental home. Some programs allow less, but a larger down payment often improves your DSCR because it lowers the monthly mortgage payment.

The lender will order an appraisal that includes a rent schedule. The appraiser estimates what the property can rent for based on comparable rentals in the Gladewater area. If the property already has a tenant, the existing lease may be used for income verification.

You will also need reserves. Most DSCR lenders want to see three to six months of mortgage payments sitting in liquid savings or investment accounts after you close.

## What Types of Properties Work with DSCR Loans

Single family rental homes are the most common fit. Duplexes, triplexes, and fourplexes work well too. Short term rental properties, the kind you list on Airbnb or VRBO, can also qualify, though the income calculation may use a different method depending on the lender.

In Gladewater, single family homes are the most popular investment vehicle. Rural properties with a few acres can qualify as well if they meet the lender's property type requirements. Properties with significant land are worth discussing with your broker upfront so there are no surprises during underwriting.

DSCR loans are for investment properties only. You cannot use one to buy your primary home.

## FAQ

**What credit score do I need for a DSCR loan in Gladewater TX?**
Most lenders require at least a 620. You will get better rates at 680 and above. Work on your score before applying if you can.

**Do I have to show tax returns or pay stubs?**
No. That is one of the biggest advantages of a DSCR loan. The lender qualifies the loan based on property income, not your personal income documents.

**Can I get a DSCR loan for a short term rental near Gladewater?**
In many cases, yes. Some lenders accept short term rental income. Just ask upfront because every lender handles this a little differently.

**How much do I need to put down?**
Most DSCR programs require 20 to 25 percent down. A larger down payment usually helps your DSCR ratio and can improve your interest rate.

**Can I have more than one DSCR loan at the same time?**
Yes. DSCR loans are popular with investors building a portfolio because personal income limits do not apply the same way they do on traditional loans.
