## What Are FHA Loan Limits in Longview TX?

An FHA loan limit is the maximum amount you can borrow using a government backed FHA loan. The FHA does not use a single national number for every buyer in the country. Instead, it reviews median home prices in each county every year and adjusts the limits to match local conditions.

Gregg County, where Longview sits, is a standard cost area. That means the loan limits here are set at the national baseline rather than elevated like some metro counties in Texas or Colorado. For most buyers in Longview, those limits are plenty. Home prices here are more affordable than in many other Texas cities, and the FHA limit gives you room to shop a solid selection of homes in Longview and nearby communities like White Oak, Kilgore, and Gladewater.

FHA also sets different limits based on how many units a property has. A single family home has one cap. A two unit duplex gets a higher cap. A three unit or four unit property gets a higher cap still. If you are open to buying a small multi unit home and living in one of the units, the FHA program can work very well for that strategy.

## How FHA Loan Limits Affect Your Buying Budget

Once you know the maximum loan amount available in Gregg County, the math is simple. Add your down payment to the loan limit and you have your purchase price ceiling. FHA loans require a minimum down payment of 3.5 percent of the purchase price when your credit score is 580 or above. If your score falls between 500 and 579, the minimum down payment rises to 10 percent, but you can still qualify.

FHA loans also require mortgage insurance. There is an upfront premium paid at closing and a monthly premium added to your payment. That cost is the trade off for getting into a home with a smaller down payment and more flexible credit requirements. For a lot of buyers in Longview, that trade off is very much worth it.

If the FHA limit covers the home you want, an FHA loan can be a smooth path forward. If you are eyeing something priced above the limit, you have options. Those options include a conventional loan, bringing a larger down payment to reduce your financed amount, or exploring a different program altogether.

## FHA Loan Limits vs. Other Mortgage Options in Longview

FHA loans are one of several strong paths buyers in Longview can take.

Conventional loans follow limits set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. If you have a solid credit score and a down payment of at least five percent, conventional can be a competitive choice. Once you reach 20 percent equity, mortgage insurance drops off, which saves money over the long run.

VA loans are available to veterans, active duty service members, and surviving spouses. With full entitlement, there is no loan limit, no required down payment, and no monthly mortgage insurance. If you or your spouse served, a VA loan is almost always the first place to look.

USDA loans cover eligible rural and suburban properties and offer no down payment financing. Some areas on the edges of Gregg County may qualify. Income limits apply, but for buyers who fit the profile, it is an excellent option.
