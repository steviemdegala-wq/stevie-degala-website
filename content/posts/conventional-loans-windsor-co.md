## What Are Conventional Loans and How Do They Work?

A conventional loan is a mortgage that is not backed by a government agency. Unlike FHA loans, which are insured by the Federal Housing Administration, or VA loans, which come with a Department of Veterans Affairs guarantee, conventional loans follow guidelines set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. That means private lenders offer them, and you work with a mortgage broker like me to find the best rate and terms for your situation.

Conventional loans come in two main types. A conforming loan stays within the loan limits set each year by the federal government. A jumbo loan goes above those limits. Windsor has seen home prices climb steadily as the Northern Colorado market has grown, so understanding where those limits land each year matters when you are planning your purchase.

## Down Payment Options for Windsor Home Buyers

One of the biggest myths about conventional loans is that you need 20 percent down. You do not. You can qualify with as little as 3 percent down. Yes, really.

Here is the trade off you should understand. If you put less than 20 percent down, you will pay private mortgage insurance, or PMI. PMI is a monthly fee added to your payment that protects the lender if you stop paying. The good news is that PMI goes away once you build enough equity in your home. With FHA loans, mortgage insurance stays for the life of the loan in most cases. That is one reason many buyers in Windsor prefer conventional loans once they have saved a solid down payment.

A 10 or 20 percent down payment removes PMI right away and keeps your monthly payment lower. If you are still building your savings, 3 to 5 percent down can still get you into a home today in a market that is not slowing down.

## Credit Score Requirements for Conventional Loans in Windsor CO

Lenders generally want to see a credit score of at least 620 to qualify for a conventional loan. To get the best interest rates, a score of 740 or higher puts you in a strong position. That said, your full financial picture matters too.

If your score is not quite there yet, a few months of focused work can make a real difference. Paying down credit card balances, avoiding new debt, and keeping all your accounts current are the fastest ways to move the needle. I can take a look at your credit report with you and point out the two or three things that would have the biggest impact before you apply.

## Understanding Your Debt to Income Ratio

Your debt to income ratio, or DTI, is how lenders measure whether your monthly income can comfortably cover your monthly debts, including your new mortgage payment. For conventional loans, most lenders want your total monthly debts to stay below 45 percent of your gross monthly income. Some programs allow up to 50 percent with strong credit and reserves.

To get a rough sense of yours, add up all your monthly debt payments, including your car loan, student loans, credit cards, and the estimated new mortgage payment, then divide by your gross monthly income. If the number feels too high, paying off a small debt before you apply can move you from a maybe to a yes pretty quickly.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Conventional Loans in Windsor CO

**Is a conventional loan better than an FHA loan for Windsor buyers?**
It depends on your situation. If you have a credit score above 680 and can put 10 percent or more down, a conventional loan usually costs less over time because the mortgage insurance eventually goes away. If your credit score is lower or your savings are limited, an FHA loan might get you approved more easily. I can run the numbers for both and show you a clear comparison.

**How long does it take to close on a conventional loan in Windsor?**
Most conventional loans close in 30 to 45 days. If your paperwork is ready and the appraisal comes back on time, we can sometimes move faster. Getting pre approved before you start house hunting is the best way to speed up the whole process.

**Can I use a conventional loan to buy a townhome or condo in Windsor?**
Yes. Condos and townhomes can qualify for conventional financing. The condo community itself does need to meet certain guidelines, so let me know what you are looking at and I can check quickly.

**What income documents will I need?**
Most buyers need two years of W2 forms, recent pay stubs from the last 30 days, and two to three months of bank statements. Self employed borrowers will need two years of tax returns. If your income situation is a little complicated, we can talk through what works best for your file.

**What if I want to buy land and build in the Windsor area?**
There are construction loan programs that convert to permanent conventional financing once your home is complete. It is a slightly different process, so let me know if that is your goal and we can map it out together.
