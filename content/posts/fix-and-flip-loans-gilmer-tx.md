## What Is a Fix and Flip Loan?

A fix and flip loan is a short term loan designed for investors who buy a property, fix it up, and then sell it for a profit. Most of these loans last between 6 and 18 months. They work very differently from a traditional 30 year mortgage. The lender cares more about the property value after repairs than your personal income history. That means even if you are self employed or have a complicated tax return, you can often still qualify based on the strength of the deal itself.

## How Fix and Flip Loans in Gilmer TX Actually Work

When you apply for a fix and flip loan, the lender focuses on two main numbers. The first is the purchase price. The second is the after repair value, which is often called the ARV. The ARV is an estimate of what the home will be worth once your renovations are complete. Most lenders will loan you a percentage of that ARV, often somewhere between 65 and 75 percent. So if a home will be worth $150,000 after repairs, a lender might loan you up to $112,500. You use those funds to buy the property and cover renovation costs. Once the work is done and you sell the home, you pay off the loan and keep the profit.

## What Lenders Look For

Fix and flip lenders focus on the deal itself, not just you as a borrower. Here are the main things they typically review.

**The property condition.** Lenders want to see that the home is genuinely fixable and that your renovation budget is realistic. A detailed scope of work from a licensed contractor makes a strong impression.

**Your experience.** If you have flipped homes before, that works in your favor. If this is your first flip, some lenders will still work with you if the deal is strong. Having a reliable contractor lined up also goes a long way toward building confidence.

**Your exit plan.** The lender wants to know how you plan to repay the loan. Usually that means selling the finished property. Having a realistic timeline and a solid plan for pricing the home matters a lot during the approval process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get a fix and flip loan if this is my first investment property?**
Yes, many lenders work with first time investors. A strong deal and a solid renovation plan go a long way. I can connect you with options that are designed for newer investors.

**How fast can I get funded?**
Fix and flip loans often close faster than traditional mortgages. Some close in as little as 10 to 14 business days, though the timeline depends on the property and the lender.

**Do fix and flip loans cover renovation costs too?**
Many do. Some lenders fund both the purchase and a portion of the renovation costs. The renovation funds are usually released in draws as work is completed and verified.

**What happens if the home does not sell before the loan is due?**
You may have options. Some lenders allow an extension for a fee. Others will let you convert to a rental loan if you decide to hold the property instead of selling. Talk through backup options with your lender before you close.

**Can I flip rural properties or acreage in Upshur County?**
Yes, though rural properties come with extra considerations like well condition, septic systems, and land access. Not every lender funds rural flips, so it is important to work with someone who knows the East Texas market well.
