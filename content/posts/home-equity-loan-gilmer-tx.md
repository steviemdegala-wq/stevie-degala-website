## What Is a Home Equity Loan in Gilmer TX?

A home equity loan is a second loan you take out alongside your existing mortgage. You borrow a set amount in one lump sum and pay it back over time at a fixed interest rate. That means your monthly payment stays the same every single month. Because your home secures the loan, the rate is usually much lower than a personal loan or credit card.

A lot of people mix this up with a cash out refinance. With a cash out refinance, you replace your current mortgage with a new, larger one. A home equity loan is completely separate. Your first mortgage stays exactly as it is, with the same rate and the same payment.

## How Much Equity Do You Have?

Your equity is the difference between what your home is worth today and what you still owe on your mortgage. If your Gilmer home is worth $190,000 and you owe $110,000, you have $80,000 in equity.

Most lenders let you borrow up to 80 or 85 percent of your home's value across all loans combined. In the example above, 80 percent of $190,000 is $152,000. Subtract the $110,000 mortgage balance, and you could borrow up to $42,000.

## Home Equity Loan vs. Cash Out Refinance in Gilmer TX

Both products let you access your equity, but they work differently. Here is a quick way to think about it.

A home equity loan makes sense when you want a lump sum at a fixed rate and you do not want to change your first mortgage. This is especially useful if your current mortgage has a low rate you want to keep.

A cash out refinance makes sense when you want to lower your first mortgage rate at the same time you pull cash out. You replace the whole loan, which means new closing costs and a new rate on the full balance.

A home equity line of credit, also called a HELOC, works like a credit card secured by your home. You draw funds as you need them rather than taking one lump sum. This is better for ongoing projects or unpredictable costs.

Talking through all three options with a mortgage broker is the fastest way to figure out which one saves you the most money.

## How to Qualify for a Home Equity Loan in Gilmer TX

Lenders look at a few key things when you apply.

Credit score. A score of 620 or above opens the most options. Higher scores usually mean lower rates.

Debt to income ratio. This is the percentage of your monthly income that goes toward paying debts. Lenders typically want this number below 43 percent, though some go higher depending on the amount of equity you have.

Home equity. You generally need to keep at least 15 to 20 percent equity in your home after the loan. The more equity you have, the easier the approval.

Employment and income. Lenders want to see steady income that supports the new payment. Self employed borrowers can usually qualify with one or two years of tax returns.

Property type. Rural properties, lots with extra acreage, and wooded parcels are common in Upshur County. Most lenders handle these without any issue, though the appraisal may take a few extra days for larger tracts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get a home equity loan in Gilmer TX with a low credit score?**

Scores below 620 make it harder, but some lenders look at the full picture including income, employment history, and total equity. It is worth having a conversation before you assume the answer is no.

**How long does it take to close a home equity loan?**

Most home equity loans close in two to four weeks. The appraisal step usually takes the longest, especially for rural properties with acreage.

**Does a home equity loan affect my existing mortgage?**

No. Your first mortgage stays exactly as it is. The home equity loan is a separate loan with its own payment.

**Are there closing costs on a home equity loan?**

Yes. Closing costs are part of the process, though they tend to be lower than a full refinance. Some lenders offer to roll the costs into the loan balance so you do not need cash out of pocket at closing.

**Can I use a home equity loan to buy investment property in East Texas?**
