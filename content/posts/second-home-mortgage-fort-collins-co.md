## What Makes a Second Home Different From a Primary Home

Lenders treat second homes a bit differently than the house you live in every day. A second home is a property you plan to use yourself for part of the year. It is not a rental you plan to list full time on a vacation platform. That distinction matters because the loan terms change depending on how you plan to use the property.

For a second home, you can still get a conventional loan with a competitive rate. You just need to meet a few extra requirements. Lenders want to see that the property is a reasonable distance from your primary home and that you plan to use it personally, not rent it out all year long.

## Down Payment for a Second Home Mortgage in Fort Collins CO

One of the first questions people ask is how much they need to put down. For a second home, most conventional loans require at least 10 percent down. Some lenders will ask for more depending on your credit score and the loan amount.

This is different from your primary home where you might qualify for 3 to 5 percent down with certain programs. Because lenders see second homes as slightly higher risk, they want a bit more equity in the deal from the start.

## What to Expect on Rates

Mortgage rates for second homes are typically a little higher than rates for primary residences. We are usually talking about a small adjustment, not a dramatic difference. The exact rate depends on your credit score, your down payment, and current market conditions.

The key thing to know is that second home rates are still lower than investment property rates. If you plan to use the home yourself and not rent it out most of the time, you benefit from better loan pricing.

## How Lenders Qualify You

When you apply for a second home mortgage in Fort Collins CO, the lender looks at your full financial picture. That includes your income, your debts, and your existing mortgage payment if you have one.

Your debt to income ratio, which is the percentage of your gross monthly income that goes toward debt payments, matters a lot here. Lenders generally want to see that number at or below 45 percent. Having two mortgage payments means that number goes up, so it helps to have solid income and low other debts.

Your credit score also plays a role. A score of 680 or higher puts you in a strong position. The stronger your score, the better the rate you can typically qualify for.

## FAQ: Second Home Mortgages in Fort Collins CO

**Can I rent out my second home sometimes and still get second home rates?**
You can rent it out occasionally, like a weekend here and there, and still qualify for second home financing. The property just needs to be primarily for your personal use. If your main plan is to rent it most of the year, lenders will treat it as an investment property with different terms.

**Do I need to live in Colorado to buy a second home in Fort Collins?**
No. You can live anywhere and purchase a second home in Fort Collins. Many buyers come from other parts of Colorado or from out of state. Your lender just needs to confirm that this property is distinct from your main residence.

**What credit score do I need for a second home loan?**
Most conventional second home loans require a score of at least 620, but you will get meaningfully better rates at 680 or above. The higher your score, the lower your rate and the lower your monthly payment.

**Can I use a VA loan for a second home?**
VA loans are designed for primary residences, so they generally cannot be used for a true second home. If you are a veteran interested in a Fort Collins property, talk to a broker about what options might still work for your situation.

**What is the difference between a second home and an investment property?**
A second home is one you use yourself for personal enjoyment for part of the year. An investment property is one you buy primarily to earn rental income. Second home loans come with lower rates and lower down payment requirements than investment property loans.
