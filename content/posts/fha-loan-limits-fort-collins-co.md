## What Are FHA Loan Limits in Fort Collins?

FHA loan limits are the maximum amount you can borrow using a government backed FHA loan. The FHA does not set one number for the entire country. Instead, it looks at median home prices county by county and adjusts limits to match local markets. For Fort Collins and the rest of Larimer County, that matters a lot. The area falls into what HUD calls a higher cost designation, which means your limit sits above the national floor. That gives you more buying power than you would have in a lower priced rural county.

FHA also sets different limits depending on the property size. A single family home has one limit, while a two unit, three unit, or four unit property gets a higher cap. If you are thinking about buying a duplex or small apartment building and living in one of the units, the FHA program can work for that too.

That yearly adjustment is how the FHA loan limit for Fort Collins stays tied to what homes actually cost here rather than a figure set years ago.

## How FHA Loan Limits Affect Your Buying Budget

Once you know the maximum loan amount available to you in Larimer County, you can work out your top purchase price. Add your down payment to your loan limit and you have a ceiling to shop within. With FHA loans, the minimum down payment is 3.5 percent of the purchase price, so you do not need to save up a large lump sum to get started.

Your credit score affects your down payment requirement. With a score of 580 or higher, you qualify for the 3.5 percent option. With a score between 500 and 579, FHA still has a path for you, but the required down payment goes up to 10 percent. If you are not sure where your credit stands, that is one of the first things to look at before you start shopping.

FHA loans also require mortgage insurance. There is an upfront premium rolled into your loan at closing, plus a monthly premium added to your payment. That is the trade off for the lower down payment and more flexible credit requirements. For many buyers, that trade off is absolutely worth it.

## FHA Loan Limits vs. Other Mortgage Options in Fort Collins

FHA loans are one of four main paths buyers in Fort Collins typically consider.

Conventional loans follow limits set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac and are often a good fit if you have a down payment of at least five percent and a strong credit score. Once you reach 20 percent equity, the mortgage insurance goes away, which is a nice long term benefit.

VA loans are available to veterans, active duty service members, and surviving spouses. They have no set loan limit for borrowers who qualify for full entitlement, no monthly mortgage insurance, and no required down payment. If you served, a VA loan is almost always the first option worth exploring.

USDA loans cover some rural and suburban areas on the edges of the Fort Collins market. Income limits apply, but the program offers no down payment financing for eligible properties and borrowers.

FHA loans shine when you have a smaller down payment, a credit score in the low to mid 600s, or you are still rebuilding after a past financial setback. Fort Collins is a competitive market, so walking in with a clear loan option already in hand makes a real difference.

**Can I use an FHA loan on a duplex or rental property in Fort Collins?**
Yes. FHA loans work on two, three, and four unit properties as long as you live in one of the units as your primary residence. The loan limit for a multi unit home is set higher than for a single family purchase.

**What credit score do I need for an FHA loan in Fort Collins?**
The FHA minimum is 500. With a score of 580 or higher, you can put down just 3.5 percent. Between 500 and 579, you will need a 10 percent down payment. Most lenders want to see your score above 580 for the smoothest path through underwriting.

**Is an FHA loan a good fit for the Fort Collins market?**
It depends on your situation. FHA is a strong option if you have a modest down payment or a score in the 580 to 660 range. If you have 20 percent saved and a score above 720, a conventional loan might save you money on mortgage insurance over the long run.

**How long does an FHA loan take to close in Fort Collins?**
Most FHA loans close in 30 to 45 days. Having your documents ready at the start, including two years of tax returns, recent pay stubs, and bank statements, keeps things moving and avoids delays.
