## What Is a Manufactured Home Loan?

A manufactured home is built in a factory and then placed on land. This is different from a modular home or a mobile home, though people often use those words interchangeably. Lenders look at two main things. First, is the home on a permanent foundation? Second, is it titled as real property (real estate) rather than personal property (like a vehicle)?

That difference matters a lot for which loan programs you can use. If your manufactured home sits on land you own and has a permanent foundation, you can often qualify for FHA loans, VA loans, USDA loans, and conventional loans. If it is on leased land or titled as personal property, your options are more limited but still exist.

## Manufactured Home Loans in Gilmer TX: Your Options

**FHA Loans**
The FHA backs loans for manufactured homes and is one of the most popular choices for buyers in Gilmer. You need at least a 580 credit score for a 3.5% down payment. The home must be on a permanent foundation and meet HUD standards. FHA is a great starting point for first time buyers who want a lower barrier to entry.

**VA Loans**
If you are a veteran or active duty service member, a VA loan is one of the strongest tools you have. VA loans often require no down payment at all. The home still needs to meet VA property standards and sit on a permanent foundation. Gilmer has a real veteran community, and this program is built for you.

**USDA Loans**
Gilmer and much of Upshur County falls within USDA eligible areas. That means qualified buyers can purchase with no down payment at all. USDA loans for manufactured homes require the home to be classified as real property on a permanent foundation. Income limits apply, so it helps to run the numbers early.

**Conventional Loans**
Conventional loans for manufactured homes are available but come with stricter requirements. You typically need a higher credit score and a larger down payment compared to government backed options. The home must be on a permanent foundation and titled as real estate. For buyers with strong credit and savings, conventional can still be a good fit.

## The Permanent Foundation Requirement

One of the most important things to understand is the permanent foundation requirement. Most loan programs need the home attached to a concrete perimeter, slab, or other fixed foundation that is secured to the ground. Homes sitting on piers or blocks alone often do not qualify for the better programs.

If the home you are considering does not have a permanent foundation, it is sometimes possible to have one installed before closing. This adds to your costs but can open up much better loan programs and lower rates. A good lender can help you think through whether that tradeoff makes sense for your situation.

## What Lenders Look At

When you apply for a manufactured home loan, lenders review several things. Your credit score matters. A score above 620 opens up more options, though some programs work with scores as low as 580. Your income and employment history are reviewed just like with any mortgage. Lenders also look at the loan-to-value ratio, which is how much you are borrowing compared to what the home is worth.

The home itself gets reviewed by an appraiser who looks at the condition, foundation type, and comparable sales nearby. Rural appraisals in Upshur County can be tricky because there are fewer recent sales to compare. Working with a lender who knows the East Texas market can make a real difference here.

## FAQ

**Can I get a manufactured home loan with a lower credit score?**
Yes, you may still have options. FHA loans allow scores as low as 580. Some programs work with scores below that, though the terms will be less favorable. The best thing to do is have a real conversation with a local broker about your specific situation.

**Does the home have to be on my own land?**
For FHA, VA, and USDA loans, yes. The home should be on land you own and on a permanent foundation. There are personal property loans called chattel loans for homes on leased land, but those come with higher rates and shorter terms.

**Can I use a USDA loan for a manufactured home in Gilmer?**
Yes. Gilmer and most of Upshur County qualifies as a USDA eligible rural area. As long as you meet income limits and the home meets property standards, USDA can be a strong fit with no down payment required.

**What is the difference between a manufactured home and a modular home?**
A manufactured home is built entirely in a factory under federal HUD standards. A modular home is also factory built but follows local building codes and is typically treated more like a traditional home for lending purposes. Modular homes often have more financing options available.

**How long does closing take for a manufactured home?**
Typically 30 to 60 days. Rural appraisals and foundation inspections can add time, so starting the process early and having your documents ready keeps things moving smoothly.
