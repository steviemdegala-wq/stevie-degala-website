Windsor and Severance sit at the northern end of the I-25 corridor in Weld County, roughly 15 to 25 minutes from Fort Collins and 20 minutes from Greeley. Both towns have experienced some of the fastest residential growth in Colorado over the past decade — driven by builders who found more land, lower lot costs, and faster permitting than what Fort Collins or Loveland could offer.

For veterans and active duty service members buying in this area, the [VA loan](/loans/va) is almost always worth running the numbers on first. The combination of zero down payment and no PMI makes a real difference in markets where home prices have moved significantly and where new construction is the primary inventory available.

## Why These Markets Have Attracted Veterans

Northern Colorado has always had a meaningful veteran population — drawn by the quality of life, the outdoor access, and proximity to employment along the Front Range. Windsor and Severance have picked up a disproportionate share of that demand because:

- New construction gives buyers modern, move-in-ready homes without competing in the resale chaos of Fort Collins
- Larger lot sizes are available at price points that are not accessible closer to the city
- The Weld County tax environment tends to be more favorable than Larimer County
- I-25 access makes commuting manageable for veterans working in Fort Collins, Greeley, Loveland, or Cheyenne

For veterans who are settling in Northern Colorado after service — or relocating from another duty station — these towns offer an entry into the Front Range market at a price point that works with a VA loan's structure.

## VA Loans for New Construction in Windsor and Severance

Most of what is available in Windsor and Severance is new construction — spec homes, build-to-order lots, and planned communities actively under development. VA loans can be used for new construction, but there are some important things to know before making an offer.

**The VA appraisal for new construction is different.** For an existing home, the VA appraisal happens once near closing. For new construction, timing matters — the VA typically wants to appraise the property at a specific stage of completion, and some lenders require the home to be 90%+ complete before they will order the appraisal. This can affect timing coordination with the builder.

**Builder warranty requirements.** VA guidelines require a one-year builder warranty on new construction. Most reputable builders in Windsor and Severance already provide this — but it needs to be confirmed in writing before closing.

**Not all builders are familiar with VA.** Some smaller or regional builders in these communities have more experience with conventional buyers than VA buyers. I flag any builder-side gaps early and communicate the VA process clearly to their team so closings do not get delayed by confusion on the construction side.

**MPR compliance on new builds.** Minimum Property Requirements for new construction are generally straightforward — new homes in permitted communities typically meet VA standards easily. The more common MPR issues show up in resale properties, not new builds.

## The Zero Down Advantage in a New Construction Market

New construction in Windsor and Severance runs roughly $450,000 to $700,000 for single-family homes depending on the community, builder, and square footage. Some premium builds exceed that range.

A veteran buying a $575,000 new build with a VA loan versus a conventional loan:

- **VA:** $0 down, no PMI, funding fee of $12,362 (at 2.15%, first use, no down payment) — which can be rolled into the loan
- **Conventional at 5% down:** $28,750 down, PMI of roughly $175–$250/month until 20% equity — potentially 8–10 years in a market with moderate appreciation
- **Conventional at 20% down:** $115,000 down, no PMI — but $115,000 tied up in equity instead of staying liquid

For most veterans in this market without $115,000 sitting in savings, VA wins on total cost before the comparison even gets complicated. For veterans with a service-connected disability rating of 10% or greater, the funding fee is waived — making the comparison even more one-sided.

## If Your Credit Score Needs Work First

Some veterans buying in Windsor or Severance are not quite at the credit threshold that makes VA lending straightforward. VA guidelines themselves do not set a minimum score — but most lenders apply overlays of 580 to 620 as a floor. For the best rates, 680 or above is where you want to be.

If your score needs work, it is worth knowing that credit can often be improved meaningfully in 60 to 90 days with targeted steps — paying down revolving balances, resolving any collections, and removing errors. I can tell you specifically what would move your score and whether waiting 60 days to reapply is worth it compared to what is available now.

## Using Your VA Benefit Multiple Times

Some veterans in this market have used their VA benefit before — on a home elsewhere, or earlier in their career. If that prior VA loan has been paid off, full entitlement is restored and can be used again. If it is still outstanding, you may have remaining partial entitlement available.

I pull your Certificate of Eligibility and calculate your entitlement position as the first step. If there is any complexity — multiple prior VA loans, remaining balances, Guard or Reserve service — I work through it directly so you know exactly where you stand before you start looking at homes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use a VA loan for a spec home under construction in Windsor?**
Yes. VA loans work for spec homes and build-to-order new construction in Windsor and Severance. The timing of the VA appraisal needs to be coordinated with the builder's construction schedule. I manage that coordination throughout the process.

**Is there a VA loan limit in Weld County?**
For veterans with full entitlement, there is no loan limit in Weld County. Since 2020, the FHFA loan limit cap no longer applies to full-entitlement VA borrowers. You can borrow as much as the lender will approve based on your income and credit.

**How long does it take to close a VA loan on new construction in Severance?**
Timeline depends on the builder's construction schedule and when the VA appraisal can be ordered. For spec homes that are 90%+ complete, the process mirrors a standard VA purchase and can close in 30 to 45 days. For build-to-order with months of construction remaining, I coordinate financing to be ready at the appropriate completion stage.

**Does my surviving spouse qualify for a VA loan to buy in Windsor?**
The unmarried surviving spouse of a veteran who died in service or from a service-connected disability is generally eligible for VA loan benefits. I confirm eligibility as the first step before discussing any property.
