## Greeley's Housing Market in Plain Terms

Greeley sits in Weld County, which has its own home price dynamics compared to Larimer County. While Fort Collins typically commands higher prices, Greeley's market has compressed in recent years, and the homes in the $400,000 to $650,000 range that are common in the area still trigger meaningful PMI costs on a conventional loan with less than 20% down.

For a medical professional buying a $525,000 home in Greeley with 5% down on a conventional loan, PMI can run $175 to $250 per month, on top of a principal and interest payment that already reflects current rates. A physician loan removes that cost entirely.

## Student Loan Debt in the Greeley Market

Conventional loan guidelines require lenders to count your student loan payment in your debt-to-income ratio. If you are on an income-driven repayment plan, some programs will use 1% of your total loan balance per month as the assumed payment, even if your actual payment is lower.

For a physician with $220,000 in student loans, that adds $2,200 per month to their calculated debt before the mortgage is even factored in. In Greeley's price range, that level of assumed student debt can knock $150,000 to $200,000 off your qualifying purchase price under conventional guidelines.

Physician loan programs treat student debt differently. Many exclude loans that are in deferment. Others use your actual IBR payment. The result is that your qualifying income stretches meaningfully further, often enough to reach the home price you are actually targeting in Weld County.

Yes. The loan qualification is based on your professional credential, not your employer type. Whether you are employed by a hospital system, a private practice, a clinic, or contracting independently, the credential is what qualifies you.

**Can I use a physician loan for new construction in the Greeley area?**
Yes. Physician loans can be used for new construction, though the process has a few additional steps compared to an existing home purchase, including a spec or pre-sale agreement and a progress inspection for some programs.
