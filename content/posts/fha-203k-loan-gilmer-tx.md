## What Is an FHA 203k Loan and How Does It Work?

An FHA 203k is a government-backed loan through the Federal Housing Administration. It is designed for homes that need repairs or updates. Instead of taking out one loan to buy the home and a separate personal loan or home equity line to pay for renovations, you combine everything into one monthly payment.

There are two versions. The Standard 203k covers major repairs like structural work, roof replacement, plumbing overhauls, or adding square footage. The Limited 203k (also called a Streamline 203k) is for smaller projects that cost $35,000 or less. That version works well for things like new flooring, kitchen updates, fresh paint, appliance upgrades, or new fixtures.

You still need to meet FHA credit and income guidelines, and the home must be your primary residence. But the threshold to qualify is generally lower than conventional loans, which makes this loan accessible for a lot of buyers in Gilmer.

## What Renovations Does the FHA 203k Cover?

The list is longer than most people expect. Under the Standard 203k you can finance structural repairs, foundation work, room additions, roofing, decks, landscaping, and energy efficiency improvements. You can also address health and safety issues like asbestos removal or lead paint remediation.

Under the Limited 203k you can cover cosmetic and non-structural upgrades. That includes new kitchen cabinets, countertops, flooring throughout the home, bathroom updates, doors, windows, appliances, HVAC replacement, insulation, and minor plumbing fixes.

One thing the loan does not cover is luxury upgrades. Things like swimming pools, outdoor kitchens, or high-end add-ons that are not considered improvements to the basic structure generally do not qualify.

## FHA 203k Loan Requirements to Know

A few key things to plan for:

You will need a minimum credit score of 580 to qualify for the low 3.5 percent down payment that FHA loans are known for. If your score is between 500 and 579, you may still qualify but would need to put 10 percent down.

The property must be a one-to-four unit home and it must be your primary residence. You cannot use this loan for a pure investment property.

You will also work with an approved HUD consultant (for the Standard version) who helps create a work plan and oversees the renovation process. The Limited version does not require a consultant but still involves a licensed contractor submitting a bid before the loan closes.

FHA loans require mortgage insurance, which is a monthly premium added to your payment. It is part of the trade-off for the lower down payment and more flexible credit requirements.

## How the Process Works Step by Step

The process has a few more moving parts than a standard home purchase, but it is manageable.

First, you get pre-approved for the 203k loan so you know your budget. Then you find a home in Gilmer that qualifies. You line up a licensed contractor to inspect the property and put together a detailed bid for the repairs. That bid gets submitted with your loan application. The lender orders an appraisal based on the future value of the home after repairs are complete. Once the loan closes, the renovation funds go into an escrow account and get released to the contractor in draws as work is completed. You move in after repairs are done or, in some cases, during the renovation if the home is livable.

The timeline is a bit longer than a standard purchase. Plan for 60 to 90 days from application to closing. But at the end of it you have a home that is exactly the way you want it, financed into one clean monthly payment.

## FAQ: FHA 203k Loans in Gilmer TX

**Can I use an FHA 203k loan to buy a manufactured home in Gilmer?**
FHA does have loan programs for manufactured homes, but the standard 203k is designed for site-built homes. If you are looking at manufactured housing, ask about other FHA loan options that may apply.

**Do I have to hire a specific contractor?**
Your contractor does not have to be on a special government list, but they must be licensed, insured, and approved by the lender before closing. The lender will review the bid before the loan is approved.

**Can I do the work myself to save money?**
Generally, no. The FHA 203k requires work to be done by licensed contractors. Owner-performed work is not allowed unless you are a licensed contractor yourself.

**How much can I borrow with an FHA 203k in Upshur County?**

**What if the repairs end up costing more than the estimate?**
